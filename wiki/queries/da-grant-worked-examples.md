Ten worked scenarios showing how the DA Grant rules apply: amounts, enterprise size, 6-month operating rule, top-ups, multiple vouchers, multiple businesses, retroactive claims, ineligible items, expiry, stopping early and reimbursement. The rules and figures are from [[da-grant]] and [[da-grant-application]].

**Rules used** (exact figures from [[official-figures]]):
- **Maximum:** $400 (or 1,600,000 Riels) formal; $250 (or 1,000,000 Riels) informal. Enterprise size (micro, small, medium) is **not** considered — tier follows registration documents.
- **Formal documents & 6-month rule:** Certificate of Incorporation or One-Window licence, and proof of **at least 6 (six) months of business operation** as of the application date (GB ¶9).
- **Informal proof:** No formal registration or 6-month registration required; requires proof of actual business operation (GB ¶9).
- **Top-ups:** above the limit, the enterprise pays the rest (GB ¶12).
- **Multiple vouchers & multiple businesses:** one business can hold more than one voucher for different solutions if the combined value stays within the maximum; an owner with multiple separate businesses can apply separately for each business (GB ¶11).
- **No retroactive claims:** only solutions bought **after** the voucher is issued can be claimed.
- **Not covered:** hardware, equipment, network infrastructure or human resources (KB §5).
- **Validity, processing, and re-application:** no fixed application deadline (depends on available funding); processing maximum 2 working days; voucher valid for up to 12 months from issuance, then expires automatically — however, if a voucher expires or is revoked, the enterprise **can re-apply** (Glossary 5.5; GB ¶12).
- **Reimbursement & progress report:** pay the DSP first; after at least 3 months of use (purchase date must be **at least 3 months before** the reimbursement request date), submit the 4-step online reimbursement request (including Efficiency, Results, Feedback + Receipt/Invoice, Purchase Date, and Proof of Payment showing recipient, date, amount, and successful status); EGD reviews and approves in **7 to 10 working days**, and the Finance team transfers the reimbursement via **KHQR** in **Khmer Riel (KHR) only** (no US dollar payments) (GB ¶17).

## 1. A registered café buys a POS system costing $600

- Registered and operating for at least 6 months → formal tier → maximum **$400 (or 1,600,000 Riels)**.
- POS is an eligible category.
- The café pays the DSP after the voucher is issued. TSC's reimbursement is capped at the voucher amount, at most $400 (paid in Khmer Riel, up to 1,600,000 Riels, via KHQR). **The café bears the remaining $200 itself** (GB ¶12).

## 2. A market-stall vendor (unregistered) wants an e-payment tool costing $180

- Informal tier → maximum **$250 (or 1,000,000 Riels)**. $180 is within the limit.
- **Does not need formal business registration or 6 months of registration.** Needs a clear purpose for the voucher and proof of actual business operation (e.g. an informal-economy recognition certificate, a permit, or a sub-national one-window certificate, GB ¶9).
- **No top-up needed.** The voucher can cover the full amount if approved.

## 3. A company registered 3 months ago wants to apply for the $400 formal grant right now

- **Not yet eligible for the formal grant ($400).** Formal (registered) applicants must hold a Certificate of Incorporation or One-Window licence **and** proof of **at least 6 (six) months of business operation** as of the application date (GB ¶9).
- Because the company was registered only 3 months ago, it must wait until it has operated for at least 6 months to apply for the formal grant. In the meantime, it can take the [[digital-adoption-assessment]] (DAA), use [[e-learning]], and join [[da-training]].

## 4. A medium-sized registered company with 120 staff asks if it gets more than $400

- **No.** Enterprise size (micro, small, medium) is **not considered**.
- The tier follows registration documents only. Every registered (formal) enterprise has the same maximum: **$400 (or 1,600,000 Riels)**. Conversely, a registered micro enterprise with 3 staff also gets up to **$400 (or 1,600,000 Riels)**.

## 5. A registered shop wants two solutions: digital marketing ($250) and inventory management ($200)

- Total $450 is more than the $400 maximum.
- Two vouchers for one business are allowed only if their **combined value stays within $400 (1,600,000 Riels)** and each is for a different digital solution (GB ¶11). For example, $250 + $150, with the shop paying the remaining $50 on inventory.

## 6. An owner has two separate businesses — a café and a salon

- **Each business can apply separately.** An owner with two or more separate businesses or shops may apply separately for each business, provided each business meets the eligibility conditions.

## 7. A business bought POS software last month before applying for the voucher

- **Cannot be claimed.** Solutions bought **before** the voucher was issued cannot be claimed. Only solutions bought **after** the voucher is officially issued can be claimed under the DA Grant.

## 8. A business wants the grant to buy a tablet and a POS app

- The **POS app** is eligible (if listed in the official platform directory).
- The **tablet** is hardware, so it isn't covered: "The DA Grant cannot be used for hardware, equipment, network infrastructure, or human resources" (KB §5; GB ¶19).

## 9. An enterprise stops using the solution after 4 months (before the voucher expires)

- The business owner **must notify TSC with clear reasons and wait for TSC's evaluation to close the payment** (GB ¶17).

## 10. How and when does the enterprise receive its reimbursement?

- Sequence: receive the voucher → buy and pay the DSP → start using the solution and submit progress reports / reimbursement request → EGD reviews and approves the reimbursement request in **7 to 10 working days** → the Finance team transfers the money via **KHQR** in **Khmer Riel (KHR) only** (no US dollar payments) (GB ¶16–17).

## Common misunderstandings

| Statement                                                         | Correct? | Why                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "EGD gives me $400 cash upfront."                                 | No       | No direct cash; reimbursement against real spending after voucher issuance (FAQ 6.3; GB ¶17)                                   |
| "Reimbursement is paid in US dollars."                            | No       | All reimbursement transactions are in **Khmer Riel (KHR) only** via **KHQR**; EGD does not pay in USD                          |
| "I bought software last month; I can claim it now."               | No       | Only solutions bought **after** the voucher is issued can be claimed                                                          |
| "A medium business with 120 staff gets more than $400."           | No       | Enterprise size (micro, small, medium) is not considered; formal cap is $400 (or 1,600,000 Riels)                             |
| "A company registered 3 months ago qualifies for the $400 grant." | No       | Formal applicants need at least **6 months** of business operation as of the application date (GB ¶9)                          |
| "The grant is a loan."                                            | No       | A voucher is a grant that "does not have to be repaid" (GB ¶5)                                                                 |
| "TSC guarantees the DSP's work."                                  | No       | TSC isn't responsible for quality or delivery; disputes are between the business and the DSP ([[da-grant-general-conditions]]) |
