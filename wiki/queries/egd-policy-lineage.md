A timeline of the documents behind EGD, from the 2021 policy framework to the 2026 chatbot knowledge base, showing what each added and how the programme's description changed.

| Date | Document | What it adds | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| May 2021 | Cambodia Digital Economy and Society Policy Framework 2021–2035 (Supreme National Economic Council) | A "Digital Businesses" pillar. Measures for free or low-cost advice to enterprises adopting digital technology, enterprise digital platforms, and public–private provision of solutions. It names the SDF and TSC as support mechanisms but doesn't name EGD. | [[digital-economy-society-policy-framework]] |
| Jan 2023 | Leadership set (stated in the Program Book §4.1) | EGD is led and coordinated by the Digital Economy and Business Committee and implemented by TSC | [[digital-economy-business-committee]] |

## How the programme's description changed

- **From roadmap to courses.** The 2023 book's "Digital Solution Roadmap" stages ("Kickstart the Digital Journey", "Integrate in the Value Chain", "Strengthen Competitive Edge") reappear as the names of DA2, DA3 and DA4 ([[da-training]]).
- **From readiness questionnaire to DAA.** The book's sector-based online readiness questionnaire became the 5-minute [[digital-adoption-assessment]].
- **The grant is newer than the book.** The 2023 book doesn't mention vouchers or the SDF. The Guidebook (v6, Nov 2024) is the earliest grant rulebook in `raw/`, and the 2026 KB added the $250 informal tier ([[source-conflicts]] #7).
- **"Five activities" vs "three supports".** The five are the 2023 design; the three are what enterprises use in 2026 ([[source-conflicts]] #1, resolved).
- **Stated but not yet visible in the sources:** the physical Enterprise Digital Transformation Center (Phnom Penh head office, provinces from 2025, Program Book §3.5). The 2026 documents give the BDC office address but don't call it the Center.
