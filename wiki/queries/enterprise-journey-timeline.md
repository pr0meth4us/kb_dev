One timeline from first login to reimbursement, with every official duration and time limit in order, and the gaps where no time is stated.

```
Day 0      CamDigiKey login → DAA (approximately 5 minutes, free)
           ↓
           Apply for a DA course (open cohort) ── approval usually 1–2 working days
           ↓
           Attend training → TSC certificate
           ↓
           Apply for DA Grant ── TSC review maximum 2 working days
           ↓   (may include a technical meeting with you and the DSP)
Voucher    Voucher issued ── valid for up to 12 months from issuance
issued     ↓
           Pay DSP, start using the solution
           ↓
+3 months  First progress report (then in stages; measurable results)
           ↓
           TSC reimburses by stage ── timing not stated
```

## Notes

- Working days follow EGD's office hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 – 12:00 and 13:30 – 17:00 ([[contacts]]). Public holidays aren't addressed in the sources.
- **Fastest possible path:**
  - DAA (minutes);
  - course approval (usually 1–2 working days);
  - grant review (maximum 2 working days).

  Course dates depend on cohorts, so the overall time from DAA to voucher depends on when a course is open.
- **Where no time is stated,** the chatbot says so and gives the contacts. It doesn't estimate.
