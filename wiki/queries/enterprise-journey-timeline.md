One timeline from taking the Digital Adoption Assessment (DAA) to DA Grant reimbursement, with every official duration and time limit in order.

```
Day 0      Take the DAA (approximately 5 minutes, free, no login required)
           ↓
           Log in with CamDigiKey → Apply for a DA course (open cohort) ── approval usually 1–2 working days
           ↓
           Attend training → TSC certificate (verifiable at verify.gov.kh)
           ↓
           Apply for DA Grant (no fixed deadline) ── TSC review maximum 2 working days
           ↓
Voucher    Voucher issued ── valid for up to 12 months from issuance
issued     ↓
           Buy & pay DSP after voucher issuance, start using the solution
           ↓
           Submit reimbursement request / progress reports (in stages, from 3 months onward)
           ↓
           EGD reviews and approves reimbursement in 7 to 10 working days
           ↓
           Finance team transfers reimbursement in Khmer Riel (KHR) via KHQR
```

| Step | Official time / rule | Page |
|---|---|---|
| DAA | Approximately 5 minutes (no login required; 4 sections, 24 scored questions + up to 7 follow-ups) | [[digital-adoption-assessment]] |
| DA Training approval | usually 1–2 working days | [[da-training-registration]] |
| Course dates | Per cohort; announced on official social media (`facebook.com/enterprisesgodigital`) and website | [[da-training]] |
| Grant application deadline | **No fixed deadline** (vouchers depend on available funding) | [[da-grant]] |
| Grant formal-track eligibility | at least 6 months of business operation as of the application date (with Certificate of Incorporation or One-Window licence) | [[da-grant]] |
| Grant application review | maximum 2 working days | [[da-grant-application]] |
| Voucher validity | valid for up to 12 months (from the date of issuance) | [[da-grant]] |
| Progress reports | in stages, from 3 months onward | [[da-grant]] |
| **Grant reimbursement review & approval** | **7 to 10 working days** (paid via KHQR in Khmer Riel only) | [[da-grant]], [[da-grant-application]] |
| E-Learning | 24/7, any time (certificate on completion, verifiable at `verify.gov.kh`) | [[e-learning]] |
| DSP registration review | **No fixed timeframe** (no set service level; refer to EGD contacts) | [[dsp-registration]] |
| Technical-support fixes | "a very short time" (no figure) | [[contacts]] |

## Notes

- Working days follow EGD's office hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 – 12:00 and 13:30 – 17:00 ([[contacts]]).
- **Important rule on purchase timing:** Only solutions bought **after** the voucher is issued can be claimed.
