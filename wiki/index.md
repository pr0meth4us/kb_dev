## organisation/

- [[egd-program]] — A free national programme, run by TSC's Enterprises Go Digital Department under the MEF, that builds the digital capabilities of Cambodian MSMEs and informal businesses.
- [[egd-department]] — The department of Techo Startup Center that implements the EGD Program.
- [[techo-startup-center]] — The public administrative institution under the MEF that implements the EGD Program through its Enterprises Go Digital Department.
- [[ministry-of-economy-and-finance]] — The ministry that oversees Techo Startup Center and the EGD Program.
- [[digital-economy-business-committee]] — The government committee that designed the EGD Program in its 2023 Program Book, and that has led and coordinated EGD since January 2023. TSC implements it.
- [[skills-development-fund]] — The fund that fully finances the EGD Program.
- [[adb-msme-digital-adoption-project]] — The ADB-funded project, 2024–2028, that the EGD Program is currently implementing.

## platform/

- [[egd-platform]] — The national one-stop digital platform of the EGD Program. It connects enterprises with certified Digital Solution Providers, digital experts, training and grants. Login is by CamDigiKey.
- [[camdigikey]] — The national digital identity system. Users need a CamDigiKey account to log in to the EGD Platform and use the DAA, DA Training and the DA Grant.
- [[platform-data-privacy]] — How the EGD Platform stores and uses enterprise data, and who can see DAA results.

## programs/

- [[digital-adoption-assessment]] — A free online self-assessment on the EGD Platform. It takes about 5 minutes and measures an enterprise's digital maturity and readiness. It is the recommended first step before DA Training or a DA Grant.
- [[da-grant]] — EGD's non-repayable grant voucher for buying a certified digital solution from a DSP, including end-user training. Up to $400 for formal enterprises and $250 for informal ones. The enterprise pays the DSP first, and TSC reimburses it after progress reports.
- [[da-training]] — EGD's free structured training programme of six activities, DA1–DA6. It helps formal and informal enterprises understand digital solutions and choose the right tools for their digital readiness level. Participants get a TSC certificate and become eligible to apply for the DA Grant.
- [[digital-solution-providers]] — Companies, startups and qualified providers that register on the EGD Platform to offer digital solutions to enterprises. Once reviewed and approved by the EGD team, they can be chosen under the DA Grant.
- [[e-learning]] — Free self-paced online video courses on the EGD Platform, available 24/7, as a complement to scheduled DA Training.
- [[trainers-and-digital-experts]] — Qualified individuals who teach DA Training and advise enterprises under the EGD Program. What they do, what's required of them, and what they gain.

- [[da5-time-to-go-digital]] — DA5: for all MSMEs and DSPs; digital literacy and links to solution providers.

- [[tourism-digital-adoption-initiative]] — Digital adoption programme for tourism MSMEs. Listed beside DA Training, but not one of DA1–DA6.

## processes/

- [[getting-started-with-egd]] — A business owner's first steps with the EGD Program: visit the platform, take the DAA, then seek support through training, E-Learning or expert consultation.
- [[request-expert-consultation]] — How an enterprise asks EGD's digital experts for direct advice: through the platform's help page, or by message on Telegram or Facebook.
- [[da-grant-application]] — Step by step: apply online on the EGD Platform, TSC reviews within 2 working days, you receive the voucher, pay the DSP, report progress, and TSC reimburses you.
- [[dsp-registration]] — How a company registers as a DSP on the EGD Platform: prepare the documents, register in the DSP section, and wait for the EGD team's review and approval.
- [[da-training-registration]] — How an enterprise registers for a DA Training course: CamDigiKey account → log in → choose a course and apply → approval, usually 1–2 working days.

## policies/

- [[digital-economy-society-policy-framework]] — The Royal Government's 15-year digital economy and society framework (May 2021). Its "Digital Businesses" pillar, which promotes digital adoption by enterprises and especially SMEs, is the part the EGD Program supports.
- [[pentagonal-strategy-phase-1]] — The Royal Government's socio-economic policy agenda for the Seventh Legislature (August 2023). Its Pentagon 5, Side 2 explicitly commits to "developing and launching the Enterprises Go Digital program".
