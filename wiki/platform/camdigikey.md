The national digital identity system. Users need a CamDigiKey account to log in to the EGD Platform and use the DAA, DA Training and the DA Grant.

**Khmer:** CamDigiKey (កម្មវិធីផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់អត្តសញ្ញាណ CamDigiKey), Glossary 2.2.

- **Definition:** "The national digital identity system required to log in and use the EGD Platform" (Glossary 2.2, 2026-09-11).
- **Required for:**
  - logging in to the [[egd-platform]] (KB §2; Brief §2.2);
  - the [[digital-adoption-assessment]], "available on the EGD Platform after logging in with CamDigiKey" (KB §3; Brief §2.3);
  - [[da-training]] registration: step 1 is "Create a CamDigiKey account" (KB §4; Brief §2.4);
  - the [[da-grant]], applied for on the platform (KB §5). The KB's grant steps don't name CamDigiKey, but a platform login is needed.
- **Website:** the enterprise page says enterprises log in with a CamDigiKey account to join the programme (web notes, 2026-10-28).

**Not in the sources:** how to create a CamDigiKey account, who issues it, which documents are needed, and whether DSPs or trainers also log in with it. CamDigiKey is not an EGD service, so the chatbot shouldn't give sign-up steps. Say a CamDigiKey account is needed and refer setup questions to [[contacts]]. See [[source-gaps]].
