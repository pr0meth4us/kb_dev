The national digital identity system used to log in to the EGD Platform for DA Training and the DA Grant, and how to create a CamDigiKey account. (Taking the Digital Adoption Assessment (DAA) does not require a CamDigiKey login.)

**Khmer:** CamDigiKey (កម្មវិធីផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់អត្តសញ្ញាណ CamDigiKey), Glossary 2.2.

- **Definition:** "The national digital identity system required to log in and use the EGD Platform" (Glossary 2.2, 2026-09-11).
- **When it is required (and when it is NOT):**
  - **Required for:** logging in to the [[egd-platform]] (KB §2; Brief §2.2), registering for [[da-training]] courses (step 1 is "Create a CamDigiKey account", KB §4), and applying for the [[da-grant]] on the platform (KB §5).
  - **NOT required for the DAA:** The [[digital-adoption-assessment]] (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/enterprise/digital-adoption-assessment`) opens directly **without a CamDigiKey or platform login**.

## How to create an account and log in

- **Create an account:** Download the **CamDigiKey app** from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). Guide: `https://camdigikey.gov.kh/` (free; one person, one account).
- **Log in to the EGD Platform:** On `enterprisedigital.gov.kh`, click **Log In** (`ចូលប្រើប្រាស់គណនី`), scan the QR code using the CamDigiKey app, and confirm with your 6-digit PIN or biometric fingerprint/FaceID.
- After logging in, enterprises register their profile at `enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/enterprise`. For help, see [[contacts]].
