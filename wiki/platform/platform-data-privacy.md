How the EGD Platform stores and uses enterprise data, and who can see DAA results.

## How data is protected (FAQ 7.1, 2026-09-11)

The FAQ states that enterprise data and confidential information entered into the [[egd-platform]] "are stored with the highest level of security and confidentiality". It lists three measures:

- **Secure storage:** all information is stored in database systems "protected with strict modern technological standards".
- **Privacy policy:** business information is "used solely for development and support service purposes within the EGD Program and will never be sold or distributed further".
- **Access control:** "only authorized professional team members can access or use that data to facilitate consultation and various support services".

## Who can see DAA results (FAQ 7.2)

Only the enterprise owner and the EGD Program technical team. See [[digital-adoption-assessment]].

## Formal policy

The website links a privacy policy (/policy) and terms (/terms) (web notes, 2026-10-28). **Neither is in `raw/`.** The chatbot shouldn't quote or summarise them, or describe specific security standards, certifications, retention periods or data-subject rights. See [[source-gaps]].

## Grant data

Under the DA Grant General Conditions (Guidebook Annex 1, Nov 2024), all data used for a grant-funded solution **belongs to the business owner**. The DSP may not use it without the owner's consent, except as the law requires. The business and the DSP share the data with TSC to evaluate the voucher's effectiveness, and TSC shares it further only with beneficiaries' consent (GB ¶18). See [[da-grant-general-conditions]].

## DSPs

For DSPs, KB §6 makes complying "with customer data security and privacy standards" a condition of registration. See [[digital-solution-providers]].
