A door-to-door digital training programme that helps entrepreneurs and MSMEs adopt digital technology in their business operations and marketing. It is a separate initiative from the six DA Training courses (DA1–DA6), gives a certificate upon successful completion, and makes participating enterprises eligible to apply for the DA Grant.

**Khmer:** Digital Technology Capacity Training (ពង្រឹងសមត្ថភាពឌីជីថលសម្រាប់សហគ្រិន), Glossary 4.9.
