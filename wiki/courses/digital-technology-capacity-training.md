A door-to-door digital training programme that helps entrepreneurs and MSMEs use digital technology in their operations and marketing. It is listed alongside DA Training on the EGD Platform, but it is not one of DA1–DA6.

**Khmer:** Digital Technology Capacity Training (វគ្គបណ្តុះបណ្តាលសមត្ថភាពបច្ចេកវិទ្យាឌីជីថល), Glossary 4.9.

- **Definition:** "A door-to-door digital training program designed to help entrepreneurs and MSMEs adopt digital technology in their business operations and marketing" (Glossary 4.9, 2026-09-11).
- **Not in the KB or the Brief.** Only the Glossary and the website mention it. See [[source-conflicts]] #5.
- **On the website** (2026-10-28; medium confidence):
  - **Path:** /digital-adoption-program/3953-digital-technology-capacity-training-cohort-1.
  - **Status:** cohort 1 was "accepting applications".
  - **Card description:** capacity building for MSMEs to expand digital adoption in business operations and marketing.
  - **Khmer title:** the site shows "ពង្រឹងសមត្ថភាពឌីជីថលសម្រាប់សហគ្រិន". That is not the Glossary term; in answers use the Glossary term.

**Answering rule:** present it as another training programme on the platform. Don't call it a DA course. Don't attach the DA1–DA6 facts to it (TSC certificate, DA Grant eligibility, 1–2 working-day approval), because the sources don't state them for this programme.

**Not in the sources:** who delivers the door-to-door visits, the target areas, the schedule, eligibility and how to register beyond the website link.
