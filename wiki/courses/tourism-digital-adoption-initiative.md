A programme to help tourism-sector MSMEs adopt digital technology, innovation and diversification to improve competitiveness. It is listed alongside DA Training on the EGD Platform, but it is not one of DA1–DA6.

**Khmer:** Tourism Digital Adoption Initiative in Cambodia (គំនិតផ្តួចផ្តើមចាប់យកឌីជីថលក្នុងវិស័យទេសចរណ៍នៅកម្ពុជា), Glossary 4.10.

- **Definition:** "A program that aims to improve the competitiveness of the private sector by supporting MSMEs in the tourism sector to adopt digital technologies, innovation, and diversification" (Glossary 4.10, 2026-09-11).
- **Not in the KB or the Brief.** Only the Glossary and the website mention it. See [[source-conflicts]] #5.
- **On the website** (2026-10-28; medium confidence):
  - **Path:** /digital-adoption-program/4202-tourism-digital-adoption-initiative-in-cambodia-cohort-2.
  - **Status:** cohort 2 was "coming soon".
  - **Khmer title:** the site shows "យុទ្ធនាការចាប់យកឌីជីថលសម្រាប់សហគ្រាសក្នុងវិស័យទេសចរណ៍" ("campaign"). That is not the Glossary term; in answers use the Glossary term.

**Answering rule:** present it as another programme on the platform, for tourism businesses. Don't attach DA1–DA6 facts to it. The sources don't name a partner such as the Ministry of Tourism, so don't name one.

**Not in the sources:** partners or funders, eligibility (which tourism businesses), activities, cohort dates and how to register beyond the website link.
