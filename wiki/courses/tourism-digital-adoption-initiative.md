A programme that supports tourism-sector MSMEs to adopt digital technology, innovation and diversification to make the private sector more competitive. It is a separate initiative from the six DA Training courses (DA1–DA6), gives a certificate upon successful completion, and makes participating enterprises eligible to apply for the DA Grant.

**Khmer:** Tourism Digital Adoption Initiative (យុទ្ធនាការចាប់យកឌីជីថលសម្រាប់សហគ្រាសក្នុងវិស័យទេសចរណ៍), Glossary 4.10.
