DA5 of EGD's six DA Training activities. Target group: All MSMEs and DSPs. The only DA course open to DSPs as well as MSMEs. It builds digital literacy and connects enterprises with digital solution providers.

**Khmer:** DA5 – Time to Go Digital (DA5 – តស់! ចាប់យកឌីជីថល!), Glossary 4.6.
