EGD's non-repayable grant voucher for buying a certified digital solution from a DSP, including end-user training. Up to $400 (or 1,600,000 Riels) for registered (formal) enterprises and $250 (or 1,000,000 Riels) for unregistered (informal) ones, regardless of enterprise size. The enterprise pays the DSP first, and TSC reimburses it in Khmer Riel (KHR) via KHQR within 7 to 10 working days after reviewing the reimbursement request.

**Khmer:** Digital Adoption (DA) Grant (ជំនួយថវិកាគាំទ្រការចាប់យកឌីជីថល (DA Grant)), Glossary 5.1. Grant Voucher (បណ្ណជំនួយថវិកា (Grant Voucher)), Glossary 5.2.

- **Chatbot documents:** the KB (§5), Brief (§2.5) and Glossary (5.x). They reflect current operational reality and are the chatbot's working text.

  - **Note:** There is no new edition of the Grant Guidebook; however, practical implementation has diverged significantly from the 2024 Guidebook. Where they differ, current operational rules govern.

## What it is

- **KB §5:** "provides financial support to help enterprises adopt digital solutions. Business owners receive a voucher that can be used to purchase certified digital tools and receive end-user training."
- **Guidebook ¶1 & ¶5:** the grant speeds up digital adoption by giving financial support in the form of a Grant Voucher — "a small grant given under set conditions that **does not have to be repaid**".
- **No cash handout:** FAQ 6.3 says "No direct cash is provided! … the EGD Program does not provide direct cash handouts". The support comes through the voucher, and TSC reimburses spending already made on an approved solution after the voucher is issued (KB §5; GB ¶17).

Goals (GB ¶2):
- promote digital adoption;
- ease the early financial burden that holds owners back;
- build owners' digital skills through the training included in the DSP's **Digital Solution Package**.

## Grant amounts and enterprise size rule

| Enterprise type | Maximum grant amount |
|---|---|
| Formal Economy (Registered) | $400 (or 1,600,000 Riels) |
| Informal Economy (Unregistered) | $250 (or 1,000,000 Riels) |

Quote these figures exactly; see [[official-figures]].

- **Enterprise size and tier:** **Micro, small, and medium size is NOT considered.** Registered status ($400 / 1,600,000 Riels formal vs $250 / 1,000,000 Riels informal) is **regardless of enterprise size or staff count**; it is based strictly on the registration documents the enterprise holds. Thus:
  - A **registered micro enterprise** gets the formal tier: up to **$400 (or 1,600,000 Riels)**.
  - A **medium company with 120 staff** also gets at most **$400 (or 1,600,000 Riels)**, not more.
- **Top-ups:** "If the cost of the selected digital solution exceeds the grant limit, the enterprise is responsible for paying the remaining amount" (KB §5; GB ¶12).

## Application rules

| Rule | Details |
|---|---|
| **Application deadline** | **No fixed deadline**; vouchers depend on available funding (GB ¶11). |
| **Solution bought before voucher issuance (retroactive claim)** | **No.** Solutions bought before the voucher was issued cannot be claimed. **Only solutions bought after the voucher is issued can be claimed.** |
| **Multiple vouchers for one business** | **Allowed:** one business may apply for **more than one voucher for different digital solutions**, provided that the **combined value of all vouchers does not exceed the maximum** ($400 formal / $250 informal) (GB ¶11). |
| **One owner with two or more separate businesses or shops** | **Allowed:** an owner with multiple separate businesses (e.g. a café and a salon) **may apply separately for each business**, if each business meets the eligibility conditions. |
| **Expired or revoked voucher** | **Can re-apply:** if a voucher expires or is revoked, the enterprise **may apply again** for a new voucher. |
| **"Other conditions apply" (GB ¶9)** | Standard administrative flexibility clause (similar to *"agenda subject to change"*), not a hidden or missing condition. |

## Validity and processing

- **Validity:** "valid for up to 12 months" (KB §5; Brief §2.5), "from the date of issuance" (Glossary 5.5; GB ¶12). An unused voucher **expires automatically** at the end of the period (GB ¶12); however, an enterprise whose voucher has expired or been revoked **can re-apply**.
- **Application processing time:** "maximum 2 working days" (KB §5; Glossary 5.6; GB ¶14).

## Eligibility conditions and required documents (GB ¶9)

### 1. Formal economy (Registered enterprises)

- **Required documents and operating history (GB ¶9):**
  1. **Certificate of Incorporation** or **One-Window licence** (ប្រកាសបង្កើតសហគ្រាស / វិញ្ញាបនបត្រចុះបញ្ជីពាណិជ្ជកម្ម ឬអាជ្ញាបណ្ណច្រកចេញចូលតែមួយ), **AND**
  2. **Proof of at least 6 (six) months of business operation** as of the application date.
  3. A **Digital Adoption Plan** is encouraged.
- **Important rule for newly registered companies (< 6 months):** If a registered company has been operating for **less than 6 months** (for example, registered 3 months ago), it is **not yet eligible** for the formal DA Grant ($400) until it has completed at least 6 months of business operation as of the application date (though it can immediately use the [[digital-adoption-assessment]], [[e-learning]], and [[da-training]]).

### 2. Informal economy (Unregistered businesses)

- **Who:** Street vendors, market stalls, small unregistered shops, and self-employed individuals (Glossary 3.5, 7.3).
- **Do they need business registration or 6 months of registration?** **No.** Unregistered (informal) businesses do **not** need formal business registration or 6 months of formal registration to apply for the **$250 (or 1,000,000 Riels)** informal grant.
- **What they need (GB ¶9):**
  1. A clear statement of the purpose of using the voucher, to strengthen and expand the business.
  2. Supporting documents proving actual business operation (e.g. an informal-economy recognition certificate, a permit, a certificate issued by the sub-national one-window office, or practical proof of business activity).

### Priority applicants (KB §5; GB ¶7)

Priority goes to enterprises that:
- have never used digital solutions before;
- need digital tools to expand their market;
- are in government priority sectors.

### Is DA Training required?

- Taking a [[da-training]] course (DA1–DA6) — or completing **[[e-learning]]**, [[digital-technology-capacity-training]], or [[tourism-digital-adoption-initiative]] — satisfies the training expectation to apply for the DA Grant and simplifies the application (GB ¶6, ¶13).
- Applicants who haven't joined a course are considered case by case based on necessity and actual assessment (GB ¶6). Recommend taking the [[digital-adoption-assessment]] and a training course or [[e-learning]] first.

## Eligible digital solutions

- **Rule:** **Every digital solution that has been reviewed, approved, and actively listed in the official EGD Platform directory (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/service-provider/solution`) is eligible for DA Grant voucher support**, including solutions listed under "Other" outside the main categories.
- **Eligible categories (Guidebook ¶12b):** Digital Marketing, E-payment, Accounting & Taxation, Point of Sale (POS), Human Resource Management, Inventory Management, E-commerce, Logistics, ERP, Chatbot for MSMEs, Customer Service Management, and Other digital solutions.
- See [[digital-solution-providers]] for the live solution directory overview and package pricing in Khmer Riel (KHR).

## What the grant can't pay for

- **Grant scope rule:** The DA Grant cannot be used for hardware, equipment, network infrastructure, or human resources.
- **GB ¶19:** beneficiaries bear their own costs of connecting to the EGD Platform and of their own IT: network infrastructure, electronic equipment and human resources. "The voucher is not used for this purpose."

## Payment and reimbursement (Guidebook ¶16–17)

1. **Buy after voucher issuance:** Only solutions bought **after** the voucher is officially issued can be claimed.
2. **Pay the DSP first:** The enterprise pays the DSP first, within the voucher's amount and validity.
3. **Use the solution for at least 3 months and submit the online Grant Reimbursement Request:**
   - Submitted online on the EGD Platform via a 4-step form:
     1. **Step 1 — Declaration:** Confirm compliance with grant conditions.
     2. **Step 2 — Experience Using the Digital Solution:** Provide qualitative progress feedback across 3 areas (Efficiency, Results, and Feedback) and attach 3 required proofs:
        - Receipt or purchase invoice for the digital solution;
        - Date of purchase (must be at least 3 months before the reimbursement application date);
        - Proof of payment showing recipient name, date, amount, and successful transaction status.
     3. **Step 3 — Bank Account Information:** Bank details for KHR transfer via KHQR.
     4. **Step 4 — Review Your Application:** Review and submit.
4. **Reimbursement review time:** EGD reviews and approves reimbursement requests in **7 to 10 working days**.
5. **Payment method and currency:** Money transfer by the Finance team to the enterprise via **KHQR in Khmer Riel (KHR) only**. No US dollar payments are made.

**Stopping early (GB ¶17):** If a business owner wants to stop using the solution before the voucher period ends, they must notify TSC with clear reasons and wait for TSC's evaluation to close the payment.

Full steps: [[da-grant-application]].

## Reporting and follow-up (GB ¶16, ¶20)

- Progress reports are due **in stages, from 3 months onward** after starting to use the solution, focusing on measurable results such as sales, productivity and costs ("ចាប់ពី ៣ ខែឡើងទៅ").
- Beneficiaries cooperate in surveys on challenges and improvements, and can receive further support after evaluation.

## Terms everyone accepts

Submitting an application means accepting the Guidebook's **General Conditions** (GB ¶13): TSC isn't responsible for the DSP's quality; the business owns its data; disputes are between the business and the DSP. See [[da-grant-general-conditions]].
