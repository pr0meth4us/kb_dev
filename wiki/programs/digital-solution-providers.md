Companies, startups and qualified providers that register on the EGD Platform to offer digital solutions to enterprises. Once reviewed and approved by the EGD team, they can be chosen under the DA Grant.

## What a DSP is

- **KB §6 (2026-09-11):** "companies, startups, or qualified providers that offer digital solutions to enterprises. They register on the EGD Platform to connect with businesses that need digital tools and services."
- **Certified / Verified DSP (Glossary 6.3):** "a Digital Solution Provider that has been reviewed and approved by the EGD team to offer solutions on the Platform".
- **Digital solution (Glossary 6.2):** software or digital tools such as POS, e-payment, accounting and e-commerce.
- **Grant role (GB ¶8, Grant Guidebook, Nov 2024):** an enterprise or startup that supplies digital solutions to business owners applying for the [[da-grant]].
- **Platform role (KB §2):** "Offer Verified Digital Products and Services". See [[egd-platform]].

## Who can become a DSP (KB §6; Brief §2.6)

The organisation must:

- be a legally registered company, enterprise, or qualified startup;
- offer clear, accurate and user-friendly digital solutions with a transparent pricing model;
- have experience in providing digital solutions;
- comply with customer data security and privacy standards;
- meet other conditions set by the EGD Program.

The website lists the same five conditions (web notes, 2026-10-28).

### Further qualities for grant-funded solutions (GB ¶10)

These are Guidebook conditions. "Preferred" (ជាការប្រសើរ) means an advantage, not a requirement.

- **Required:** quality, safe solutions that meet digital-technology standards and the law in force.
- **Preferred:** experience serving enterprises of all sizes, especially MSMEs in Cambodia.
- **Preferred:** able to offer a **discount** to business owners buying with a Grant Voucher.
- **Preferred:** a **Khmer-language user interface (UI)**.
- Other conditions apply.

## Benefits of joining (KB §6; Brief §2.6)

- Collaborate on projects under the EGD Program.
- Expand market reach by connecting with enterprises seeking digital solutions.
- Showcase digital products and innovations on the national platform.
- Network with businesses, government agencies and other stakeholders.
- Take part in knowledge-sharing sessions, forums and events.
- Contribute to the digital transformation of Cambodian enterprises.

The website lists 8 benefits (web notes). Its two extras are partnering with the programme under the [[digital-economy-society-policy-framework]], and taking part in a [[techo-startup-center]] programme. They add nothing material; answer with the KB's six.

**No fee:** EGD charges DSPs no enrolment fees or commissions (KB §1).

## DSPs and training

- [[da5-time-to-go-digital]] is the one DA course whose target group includes DSPs ("All MSMEs and DSPs", KB §4).
- DA Training is delivered by "industry experts and certified DSPs" (KB §4).
- The Grant Guidebook (GB ¶6) says both beneficiary types, business owners and DSPs, are expected to take part in an EGD digital adoption course.

## DSPs under the DA Grant

- **Payment:** the business owner pays the DSP first. TSC reimburses the owner, not the DSP (KB §5; GB ¶17).
- **Contract:** the DSP and the business sign their own contract. **TSC isn't responsible** for the solution's quality or delivery, and doesn't decide disputes between them ([[da-grant-general-conditions]], conditions 1, 5, 6).
- **Data:** the business owns all data used in the solution. The DSP may not use it without consent, except as the law requires (condition 3).
- **Monitoring:** the DSP cooperates with TSC's monitoring of the voucher's use and impact, and may join a technical meeting before an application is approved (GB ¶14, ¶19).
- **Neutrality:** TSC lists solutions but **does not push any particular DSP or solution** (GB ¶18).

## How enterprises find DSPs (FAQ 3.4)

1. Browse the directory and profiles of DSPs on the EGD Platform. The solution directory is at /ecosystem/service-provider/solution (web notes).
2. Choose the type of service or technology needed (e.g. business management systems, POS, digital marketing).
3. Contact providers directly through the platform.
