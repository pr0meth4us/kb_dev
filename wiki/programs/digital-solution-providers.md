Companies, startups and qualified providers that register on the EGD Platform to offer digital solutions to enterprises. Once reviewed, approved, and actively listed in the official EGD Platform directory (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/service-provider/solution`), their solutions are eligible for DA Grant voucher support.

**Khmer:**
- Digital Solution Provider (DSP) (អ្នកផ្តល់ដំណោះស្រាយឌីជីថល (DSP)), Glossary 6.1.
- Digital Solution (ដំណោះស្រាយឌីជីថល), 6.2.
- Certified / Verified DSP (DSP ដែលមានការទទួលស្គាល់ / បានផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់), 6.3.
- Portfolio of Digital Solutions (បញ្ជីស្នាដៃ / បញ្ជីដំណោះស្រាយឌីជីថល), 6.5.

## What a DSP is

- **KB §6 (2026-09-11):** "companies, startups, or qualified providers that offer digital solutions to enterprises. They register on the EGD Platform to connect with businesses that need digital tools and services."
- **Certified / Verified DSP (Glossary 6.3):** "a Digital Solution Provider that has been reviewed and approved by the EGD team to offer solutions on the Platform".
- **Digital solution (Glossary 6.2):** software or digital tools such as POS, e-payment, accounting and e-commerce.
- **Grant role (GB ¶8, Grant Guidebook, Nov 2024):** an enterprise or startup that supplies digital solutions to business owners applying for the [[da-grant]].
- **Platform role (KB §2):** "Offer Verified Digital Products and Services". See [[egd-platform]].
- **Responsible office:** DSP registration and the platform are handled by the **Digital Capture Office** (ការិយាល័យការចាប់យកឌីជីថល) of the [[egd-department]].

## Registration requirements, approval, and operating conditions

- **Requirements and documents do not apply in practice:** The requirements in the Grant Guidebook and the document lists in earlier KB drafts do not apply in practice. The chatbot does not list requirements or required registration documents; applicants are referred directly to the EGD team through [[contacts]].
- **Approval timeframe:** There is **no fixed timeframe** (no set service level). For application status or review timelines, applicants are referred to the EGD team ([[contacts]]).
- **Status validity:** Approved DSP status **does not expire** unless the DSP requests to be removed or delisted from the platform.
- **Foreign companies:** A foreign company may register as a DSP provided it has **operations in Cambodia or a licensed registration in Cambodia**.
- **Login and profile management:** DSPs log in via [[camdigikey]] and manage their profile information at `/user/profile/info` on the EGD Platform.

See [[dsp-registration]] for how to apply on the platform.

## Benefits of joining as a DSP (KB §6; Brief §2.6)

- Collaborate on projects under the EGD Program.
- Expand market reach by connecting with enterprises seeking digital solutions.
- Showcase digital products and innovations on the national platform.
- Network with businesses, government agencies and other stakeholders.
- Take part in knowledge-sharing sessions, forums and events.
- Contribute to the digital transformation of Cambodian enterprises.

**No fee:** EGD charges DSPs no enrolment fees or commissions (KB §1).

## DSPs and training

- [[da5-time-to-go-digital]] is the one DA course whose target group includes DSPs ("All MSMEs and DSPs", KB §4).
- DA Training is delivered by "industry experts and certified DSPs" (KB §4). Trainers are DSPs registered on the EGD Platform and subject matter experts ([[trainers-and-digital-experts]]).

## DSPs under the DA Grant

- **Payment:** the business owner pays the DSP first. TSC reimburses the owner (in KHR via KHQR), not the DSP (GB ¶17).
- **Contract:** the DSP and the business sign their own contract. **TSC isn't responsible** for the solution's quality or delivery, and doesn't decide disputes between them ([[da-grant-general-conditions]], conditions 1, 5, 6).
- **Data:** the business owns all data used in the solution. The DSP may not use it without consent, except as the law requires (condition 3).
- **Monitoring:** the DSP cooperates with TSC's monitoring of the voucher's use and impact, and may join a technical meeting before an application is approved (GB ¶14, ¶19).
- **Neutrality:** TSC lists solutions but **does not push or recommend any particular DSP or solution** (GB ¶18).
