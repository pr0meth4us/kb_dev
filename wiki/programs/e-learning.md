Free self-paced online video courses on the EGD Platform, available 24/7, as a complement to scheduled DA Training. Learners receive a certificate upon completing a course, which can be verified online at `verify.gov.kh`.

**Khmer:** E-Learning (កម្មវិធីស្វ័យសិក្សា (E-Learning)), Glossary 4.8. The FAQ calls it "ថ្នាលកម្មវិធីស្វ័យសិក្សា" ("Self-Paced Learning platform"); use the Glossary form.

- **Definition:** "Self-paced online courses available 24/7 on the EGD Platform" (Glossary 4.8; KB §4, 2026-09-11).
- **How it works (FAQ 2.1):** sign up on the EGD Platform, then "access and watch video lessons on the Self-Paced Learning platform anytime and anywhere". There are no scheduled dates, unlike [[da-training]].
- **Certificate, verification, and DA Grant eligibility:**
  - **Does E-Learning give a certificate?** **Yes**, learners receive a certificate on completing the course.
  - **How to verify an E-Learning certificate:** it can be checked and verified online at **`verify.gov.kh`**.
  - **Does completing E-Learning alone satisfy the DA Grant training expectation?** **Yes** — completing E-Learning satisfies the training expectation for applying for the [[da-grant]].
- **Main function:** accessing E-Learning courses is one of the platform's main functions for enterprises (KB §2; see [[egd-platform]]).
- **Free:** EGD E-Learning costs nothing (FAQ 3.3).
- **Good for new businesses:** FAQ 6.1 suggests E-Learning to strengthen knowledge of enterprise management and digital technology.
- **Where to access & course catalogue:**
  - **Direct LMS URL:** **`https://lms.enterprisedigital.gov.kh/`** (the website menu also links to self-study video lessons at `/resources/training-material`).
  - **Course list:** the specific E-Learning course catalogue is continuously updated and expanded as part of ongoing platform improvement; users can browse the current courses directly at `https://lms.enterprisedigital.gov.kh/`.
