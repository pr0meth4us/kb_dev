Free self-paced online video courses on the EGD Platform, available 24/7, as a complement to scheduled DA Training.

**Khmer:** E-Learning (កម្មវិធីស្វ័យសិក្សា (E-Learning)), Glossary 4.8. The FAQ calls it "ថ្នាលកម្មវិធីស្វ័យសិក្សា" ("Self-Paced Learning platform"); use the Glossary form.

- **Definition:** "Self-paced online courses available 24/7 on the EGD Platform" (Glossary 4.8; KB §4, 2026-09-11).
- **How it works (FAQ 2.1):** sign up on the EGD Platform, then "access and watch video lessons on the Self-Paced Learning platform anytime and anywhere". There are no scheduled dates, unlike [[da-training]].
- **Main function:** accessing E-Learning courses is one of the platform's main functions for enterprises (KB §2; see [[egd-platform]]).
- **Free:** EGD E-Learning costs nothing (FAQ 3.3).
- **Good for new businesses:** FAQ 6.1 suggests E-Learning to strengthen knowledge of enterprise management and digital technology.
- **On the website:** the menu item for self-study video lessons is /resources/training-material (web notes, 2026-10-28; lower confidence).

**Not in the sources:**
- the course list and topics;
- languages;
- whether E-Learning earns a certificate. The TSC certificate is stated only for DA Training.
- whether it counts towards DA Grant eligibility.

Don't say it does either; see [[source-gaps]].
