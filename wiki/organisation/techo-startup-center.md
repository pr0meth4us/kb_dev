The public administrative institution under the MEF that implements the EGD Program through its Enterprises Go Digital Department.

**Khmer:** Techo Startup Center (TSC) (មជ្ឈមណ្ឌលបណ្តុះធុរកិច្ចថ្មី «តេជោ» (ម.ធ.ត.)), Glossary 1.2.

- **Status:** "A public administrative institution under the Ministry of Economy and Finance that implements the EGD Program" (Glossary 1.2, 2026-09-11). Parent ministry: [[ministry-of-economy-and-finance]].
- **Runs EGD** through the [[egd-department]] (KB §1; Glossary 1.5).
- **Roles in the programme** stated in the sources:
  - issues the **official certificate** for DA Training (KB §4; see [[da-training]]);
  - **reimburses the enterprise** under the DA Grant after implementation and a progress report, and receives the progress reports (KB §5; see [[da-grant]]);
  - under the Grant Guidebook (Nov 2024, ¶18; see [[da-grant-general-conditions]]), it:
    - accepts or rejects grant applications at its discretion;
    - lists the platform's solutions without pushing any particular DSP;
    - shares voucher data only with beneficiaries' consent;
    - keeps the Guidebook up to date;
    - acts as **facilitator**, liable only for reimbursement, not for DSP quality or disputes;
  - its official channels carry EGD news (FAQ 5.3).
- **Email:** EGD's official contact email is on TSC's domain, info@techostartup.center (Brief §2.1; KB §7.1). See [[contacts]].
- **Website:** https://techostartup.center/. The EGD site links to it (web notes, 2026-10-28; low–medium confidence).

TSC's other programmes are out of scope for this wiki.
