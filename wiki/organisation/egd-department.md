The department of Techo Startup Center that implements the EGD Program, operating through two main offices: the Policy Research Office and the Digital Capture Office.

**Khmer:** Enterprises Go Digital Department (នាយកដ្ឋានបរិវត្តកម្មឌីជីថលសហគ្រាស), Glossary 1.5.

- **Role:** "The department under Techo Startup Center responsible for implementing the EGD Program" (Glossary 1.5, 2026-09-11). Also stated in KB §1 and FAQ 1.1 and 1.3.
- **Parent body:** [[techo-startup-center]], under the [[ministry-of-economy-and-finance]].
- **Implements:** [[egd-program]], including the [[egd-platform]], [[da-training]], [[da-grant]] and DSP registration ([[digital-solution-providers]]).
- **Leadership above TSC:** the [[digital-economy-business-committee]] leads and coordinates EGD (Program Book §4.1).
- **Contact:** see [[contacts]].

## Offices of the EGD Department

The EGD Department has two offices established under the Prakas on the Organization and Functioning of Offices under the Departments of Techo Startup Center, Ministry of Economy and Finance (2024):

| Office (English) | Office (official Khmer) | Duties |
|---|---|---|
| **Policy Research Office** | ការិយាល័យស្រាវជ្រាវគោលនយោបាយ ([[glossary]] 1.6) | Researches, collects and analyses data and prepares reports on enterprise digital transformation and the digital economy and society; collects inputs and compiles supporting documents; gives policy recommendations; helps coordinate national and international research cooperation (Article 15). |
| **Digital Capture Office** | ការិយាល័យការចាប់យកឌីជីថល ([[glossary]] 1.7) | Plans and delivers training and digital adoption support for enterprises; proposes, runs and evaluates promotion; coordinates national and international cooperation on training and knowledge sharing; plans resources for, delivers and evaluates government and partner-supported projects (Article 14). |

### Responsibilities of the Digital Capture Office

The Digital Capture Office is the operational arm that handles:
- **Training and digital adoption support** for enterprises ([[da-training]], [[e-learning]]).
- **Promotion** and awareness activities.
- **National and international cooperation** on training and knowledge sharing.
- **Government and partner-supported projects**.
- **The [[da-grant]]**, **DSP registration** ([[dsp-registration]], [[digital-solution-providers]]), and **the [[egd-platform]]** (Prakas on TSC Offices 2024, Article 14).

The department's internal approval workflow and staff-only procedures are kept on internal pages, not here.
