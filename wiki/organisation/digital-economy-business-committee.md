The government committee that designed the EGD Program in its 2023 Program Book, and that has led and coordinated EGD since January 2023. TSC implements it.

**Khmer** (from the Program Book; not a Glossary term): គណៈកម្មាធិការសេដ្ឋកិច្ច និង ធុរកិច្ចឌីជីថល (គ.ស.ធ.ឌ.). There is no Glossary entry; see [[glossary]]. In Khmer answers, use this name as the Program Book writes it.

- **Author of the Program Book:** the EGD Program Book ("កម្មវិធីបរិវត្តកម្មឌីជីថលសហគ្រាស – ENTERPRISES GO DIGITAL PROGRAM", 2023) was "prepared by" the committee (cover). Copyright belongs to its General Secretariat (p. 2).
- **Chair:** the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Economy and Finance. As Chair, he signed the Program Book's foreword in Phnom Penh on 21 April 2023 (pp. 4–5). The foreword says the Government, "through the Digital Economy and Business Committee", launches the EGD Program. See [[ministry-of-economy-and-finance]].
- **Role in EGD (Program Book §4.1):** "from January 2023", the EGD Program is "led and coordinated by" the committee. Its **General Secretariat** acts as **technical staff**. The programme is **implemented by [[techo-startup-center]]**, working with relevant ministries and institutions.
- **Programme sustainability (§3.6):** the General Secretariat, which leads and coordinates the programme, will study a business model or business plan that could give EGD its own revenue, after an evaluation and once measurable results exist.
- **Address (p. 2):** BDC building, **17th floor**, OCIC Boulevard, Chroy Changvar, Phnom Penh. EGD's own office is on the 11th floor of the same building ([[contacts]]).
- **Website:** digitaleconomy.gov.kh (p. 2). Its publications page hosts the Program Book and the [[digital-economy-society-policy-framework]].

**How this fits the 2026 chatbot documents:** the KB and Brief describe EGD as implemented by TSC's [[egd-department]] under the MEF, and don't mention the committee. That is consistent: the committee leads and coordinates, and TSC implements. Answer "who runs EGD?" with the KB's wording, and mention the committee when asked about leadership or policy ownership.
