The Asian Development Bank (ADB) Technical Assistance project (**TA 10365-CAM** / Project Number **55255-004**, 2024–2028) anchored within the EGD Program at Techo Startup Center (TSC), funded by the **Government of Japan** via the **Japan Special Fund (JSF)**.

**Khmer:** no official Glossary term (see [[glossary]]).

## Key project details

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| **Official project name** | *Supporting the Adoption of Digital Technologies and Fostering Innovation in Micro, Small, and Medium-Sized Enterprises* |
| **ADB TA & Project Number** | **TA 10365-CAM** (ADB Project Number **55255-004**, Contract `S206710`) |
| **Funder & donor** | Funded by the **Government of Japan** via the Asian Development Bank's **Japan Special Fund (JSF)** (in coordination with the Embassy of Japan and JICA / CJCC). **Not funded by the [[skills-development-fund]] (SDF)** — SDF funds the core [[egd-program]] under MEF, whereas `TA 10365-CAM` is an independent Japanese-funded ADB technical assistance project anchored within EGD at [[techo-startup-center]]. |
| **Total grant budget** | **USD $1.35 million** (Japan Special Fund) |
| **Period & campaigns** | **2024–2028** (approved 28 October 2024; 37-month implementation from August 2025 to August 2028 across **three 12-month sector campaigns** under the **Digital Adoption Initiative [DAI]**:<br>• **Campaign 1 (Year 1, Oct 2025 – Sep 2026):** Tourism MSMEs ([[tourism-digital-adoption-initiative]])<br>• **Campaign 2 (Year 2, Sep 2026 – Aug 2027):** Food-processing / priority sector<br>• **Campaign 3 (Year 3, Aug 2027 – Jul 2028):** Third priority sector) |
| **Implementing body** | Implemented under the [[egd-program]] by the [[egd-department]] of [[techo-startup-center]] (with technical consulting by Clickable Impact). |

## Roles of the key players (`TA 10365-CAM`)

| Player | Role in the Project | Key Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Government of Japan** *(Donor / Funder)* | **Financial Donor** via ADB's **Japan Special Fund (JSF)** (**USD $1.35 million**). | • Provides the $1.35 million grant funding through ADB trust funds.<br>• **Embassy of Japan** (Economic & ODA Section) receives quarterly progress reports, attends major DAI launch/outreach events, and receives donor visibility/brand recognition in DAI communications.<br>• Coordinates bilateral synergies with **JICA** and the **Cambodia-Japan Cooperation Center (CJCC)** (a technical partner for localized Khmer classroom training and MSME mentoring). |
| **Asian Development Bank (ADB)** *(Administering & Supervising Agency)* | **TA Administrator & Oversight Agency** (`TA 10365-CAM` / Project `55255-004`, Public Sector Management and Governance Sector Office). | • Administers the Japan Special Fund grant, approves the TA design and Design & Monitoring Framework (DMF), and contracts the technical assistance consulting team (`Contract S206710`).<br>• Supervises project implementation, reviews Quarterly Reports and Results Management Plans (RMPs), and oversees KPI and budget compliance. |
| **Techo Startup Center (TSC)** *(Lead Implementing Agency & Long-Term Custodian)* | **Primary Actor, Implementing Agency & Owner** (under the [[ministry-of-economy-and-finance]], via the [[egd-department]]). | • Anchors the **Digital Adoption Initiative (DAI)** within the [[egd-program]] and leads implementation, partnership management, and MSME engagement.<br>• Hosts the DAI systems, automated Digital Diagnostic tool, and Knowledge Hub (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh`).<br>• Nominates internal focal points across 7 areas (overall management, M&E, gender, research & digital diagnostics, training, awareness/communications, and partnerships) under a **progressive ownership model** — co-implementing in Campaign 1 and assuming full operational control in Campaigns 2 and 3 and beyond the 37-month TA. |
| **Clickable Impact** *(Technical Assistance Consultant)* | **TA Consulting Firm** (`Contract S206710`, 37 months, Aug 2025 – Aug 2028). | • Mobilizes national and international experts (in MSME digitalization, gender, communications, capacity building, and results management) to **support, advise, and supplement TSC's capacity**.<br>• Co-authors the living **Campaign Manual & SOPs** and **Digital Adoption Framework (DAF)** with TSC.<br>• Co-designs and pilots the **automated Digital Diagnostic & Digital Adoption Plan** tool (testing with 400 MSMEs/campaign = 1,200 total), conducts sector/gender research and the Training Needs Assessment (TNA), facilitates partnerships and events (heavily in Campaign 1, tapering in Campaigns 2–3), and submits Quarterly and Post-Campaign Reports to ADB. |

## Outputs and numerical targets (KPIs)

1. **Output 1 — Institutional Capacity of TSC:** Training Needs Assessment (TNA), Campaign Manual & SOPs, Sector & Gender Research (with **at least 40% women's participation**), and Results Management Plans.
2. **Output 2 — Awareness & Partnerships:** MSME Champions Program, badging/certification, **6 formal partnership agreements** with sector associations (e.g. CATA, CTF, CWEA, CYEA, CJCC, Khmer Enterprise), and **at least 8 knowledge-sharing and networking events** (expanded in the work plan to **13 events per campaign [8 in-person, 5 online] / 39 total**) with **at least 35% women's participation**.
3. **Output 3 — Improved Access to Digital Solutions:** Digital Adoption Framework (DAF), enhancements to the EGD Knowledge Hub (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh`), DSP vetting prioritizing Cambodian DSPs, and DSP onboarding/e-learning materials.
4. **Output 4 — Diagnostics & Digital Adoption Plans:**
   - **1,000 MSME applications** through open calls;
   - **500 digital diagnostics completed** (1,200 including pilot testing at 400 per campaign);
   - **500 Digital Adoption Plans (DAPs) prepared**, with **at least 250 implemented**.
