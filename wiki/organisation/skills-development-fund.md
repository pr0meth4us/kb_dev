The fund that fully finances the EGD Program.

**Khmer:** Skills Development Fund (SDF) (មូលនិធិអភិវឌ្ឍន៍ជំនាញ (SDF)), Glossary 1.4.

- "The fund that fully finances the EGD Program" (Glossary 1.4, 2026-09-11). KB §1 and Brief §2.1 say EGD is "fully funded by the Skills Development Fund (SDF)".
- That's why EGD charges no enrolment fees or commissions (KB §1). See [[egd-program]].
- The EGD website footer shows a funding logo whose file name contains "SDF" (web notes, 2026-10-28; low confidence).

The sources don't say who administers the SDF, so the chatbot shouldn't name an administering body.

**Not in the sources:** how the "fully funded by SDF" statement fits with the ADB-funded project ([[adb-msme-digital-adoption-project]]). Don't answer questions about funding shares; refer them to [[contacts]].
