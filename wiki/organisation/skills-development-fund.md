The fund that fully finances the core EGD Program under the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF).

**Khmer:** Skills Development Fund (SDF) (មូលនិធិអភិវឌ្ឍន៍ជំនាញ (SDF)), Glossary 1.4.

- "The fund that fully finances the EGD Program" (Glossary 1.4, 2026-09-11). KB §1 and Brief §2.1 say EGD is "fully funded by the Skills Development Fund (SDF)".
- That's why EGD charges no enrolment fees or commissions (KB §1). See [[egd-program]].
- The EGD website footer shows a funding logo whose file name contains "SDF" (web notes, 2026-10-28; low confidence).
- **How SDF funding fits with the ADB MSME Digital Adoption project ([[adb-msme-digital-adoption-project]]):**
  - **SDF finances the core [[egd-program]]** (under the [[ministry-of-economy-and-finance]]).
  - **SDF does NOT fund the ADB project.** The ADB project (**TA 10365-CAM** / Project Number **55255-004**: *Supporting the Adoption of Digital Technologies and Fostering Innovation in Micro, Small, and Medium-Sized Enterprises*, USD $1.35 million) is an independent Asian Development Bank technical assistance initiative funded by the **Government of Japan** via the **Japan Special Fund (JSF)** and anchored within EGD at [[techo-startup-center]].
