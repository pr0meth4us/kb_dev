A free national programme, run by TSC's Enterprises Go Digital Department under the MEF, that builds the digital capabilities of Cambodian MSMEs and informal businesses.

**Khmer:** Enterprises Go Digital (EGD) Program (កម្មវិធីបរិវត្តកម្មឌីជីថលសហគ្រាស (EGD)), Glossary 1.1.

## Who runs it

- **Implemented by** the [[egd-department]] of [[techo-startup-center]] (TSC), under the [[ministry-of-economy-and-finance]] (MEF). Sources: KB §1; Project Brief §2.1; Glossary 1.1–1.5. All 2026-09-11.
- **An MEF initiative:** FAQ 1.3 calls EGD "an initiative of the Ministry of Economy and Finance". It also receives "cooperation and support from various state institutions as well as national and international development partners". None are named.
- **Funding:** "Fully funded by the Skills Development Fund (SDF)" ([[skills-development-fund]]). Sources: KB §1; Brief §2.1; Glossary 1.4.
- **Current donor project:** EGD is implementing the ADB-funded project "Supporting the Adoption of Digital Technologies and Fostering Innovation in Micro, Small, and Medium-Sized Enterprises" (2024–2028). See [[adb-msme-digital-adoption-project]]. Sources: KB §1; Brief §2.1.

## Purpose

- **KB §1:** EGD "aims to strengthen the digital capabilities of Cambodian enterprises so they can play a more active role in the digital economy and business". It supports MSMEs and informal economy actors "through training, digital tools, and financial support".
- **Brief §2.1:** the same purpose, with the support described as "training, digital tools, and financial grant vouchers".
- **FAQ 1.2** lists three objectives:
  - **Promoting Digital Adoption:** helping enterprises at all levels integrate digital technology into daily operations.
  - **Building Human Resource Capacity:** through DA Training and self-paced learning.
  - **Connecting Relationships:** bridging enterprises, digital experts and development partners.

## Key facts

- **Non-profit** public administrative initiative (KB §1; Brief §2.1).
- **No fees:** "No enrollment fees or commissions are charged to enterprises or Digital Solution Providers (DSPs)" (KB §1). FAQ 3.3 says participation and all support features are "completely free of charge".
- **Policy alignment:** the "Digital Business" pillar of the [[digital-economy-society-policy-framework]] 2021–2035, and Pentagon 5 (Side 2) of the [[pentagonal-strategy-phase-1]] (KB §1; Brief §2.1).
- **Open to new businesses:** startups and newly established businesses "can fully participate or register" (FAQ 6.1).

## What it offers

The sources describe the offer in two ways that haven't been reconciled.

- **"Five priority activities":** KB §1 and Brief §2.1 say EGD "has five priority activities". Neither lists them; both defer to the **EGD Program Book**, which is not in `raw/` ([[source-gaps]]).
- **Three core supports:** FAQ 1.1 and 1.4 list three core activities or supports:
  1. [[da-training]], with "6 specialized training activities";
  2. the [[da-grant]];
  3. the [[egd-platform]], a central gateway to development partners and official DSPs.

> **Conflict (open):** "five priority activities" (KB, Brief) vs "three core activities" (FAQ). Until the Program Book is in `raw/`, answer with the three named supports and don't state a count of five. Logged on [[source-conflicts]].

The platform's enterprise journey, from the KB and Brief:

```
CamDigiKey login → DAA (≈5 min, free) → DA Training (DA1–DA6) → DA Grant voucher → certified DSP solution
                                       ↘ E-Learning (self-paced, 24/7)
```

Pages: [[camdigikey]], [[digital-adoption-assessment]], [[da-training]], [[e-learning]], [[da-grant]], [[digital-solution-providers]].

Beyond DA1–DA6, the Glossary (4.9, 4.10) defines two more training offers: the **Digital Technology Capacity Training** (door-to-door) and the **Tourism Digital Adoption Initiative in Cambodia**. The live site listed both on 2026-10-28 (web notes, low–medium confidence). The KB and Brief don't mention them. They are ingested with DA Training in batch 3.

## Benefits for business owners (FAQ 3.1)

- **Knowledge and skills:** through [[e-learning]] and [[da-training]].
- **Digital Adoption Grants:** ease financial burdens and speed up adoption ([[da-grant]]).
- **Digital experts:** consulting, guidance and practical solutions ([[request-expert-consultation]]).
- **Partners and solutions:** the [[egd-platform]] as a gateway to suitable [[digital-solution-providers]].

## Who it serves

- **MSMEs**, formal and informal ("the main target of the EGD Program", Glossary 7.1).
- **Digital Solution Providers** ([[digital-solution-providers]]).
- **Development partners**.
- **Trainers and digital experts**.

These are the Brief's public user groups (§1.2), and the KB's "Key Users" table (§2) lists the same groups.
