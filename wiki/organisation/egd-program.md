A free national programme, run by TSC's Enterprises Go Digital Department under the MEF, that builds the digital capabilities of Cambodian MSMEs and informal businesses.

**Khmer:** Enterprises Go Digital (EGD) Program (កម្មវិធីបរិវត្តកម្មឌីជីថលសហគ្រាស (EGD)), Glossary 1.1.

## Who runs it

- **Implemented by** the [[egd-department]] of [[techo-startup-center]] (TSC), under the [[ministry-of-economy-and-finance]] (MEF). The EGD Department operates through two offices: the **Policy Research Office** (ការិយាល័យស្រាវជ្រាវគោលនយោបាយ) and the **Digital Capture Office** (ការិយាល័យការចាប់យកឌីជីថល). Sources: KB §1; Project Brief §2.1; Glossary 1.1–1.7; Prakas on TSC Offices 2024.
- **An MEF initiative:** FAQ 1.3 calls EGD "an initiative of the Ministry of Economy and Finance". It also receives "cooperation and support from various state institutions as well as national and international development partners". None are named.
- **Funding:** The core EGD Program is "fully funded by the Skills Development Fund (SDF)" ([[skills-development-fund]]) under MEF. Sources: KB §1; Brief §2.1; Glossary 1.4.
- **Current donor project:** EGD is also implementing the independent ADB Technical Assistance project **TA 10365-CAM** (Project Number **55255-004**): "Supporting the Adoption of Digital Technologies and Fostering Innovation in Micro, Small, and Medium-Sized Enterprises" (2024–2028), which is **not funded by SDF** but is funded by the **Government of Japan** via ADB's **Japan Special Fund (JSF)** (**USD $1.35 million**). See [[adb-msme-digital-adoption-project]]. Sources: KB §1; Brief §2.1; ADB Project Data Sheet.

## Purpose

- **KB §1:** EGD "aims to strengthen the digital capabilities of Cambodian enterprises so they can play a more active role in the digital economy and business". It supports MSMEs and informal economy actors "through training, digital tools, and financial support".
- **Brief §2.1:** the same purpose, with the support described as "training, digital tools, and financial grant vouchers".
- **FAQ 1.2** lists three objectives:
  - **Promoting Digital Adoption:** helping enterprises at all levels integrate digital technology into daily operations.
  - **Building Human Resource Capacity:** through DA Training and self-paced learning.
  - **Connecting Relationships:** bridging enterprises, digital experts and development partners.

## Key facts

- **Non-profit** public administrative initiative (KB §1; Brief §2.1).
- **No fees:** "No enrollment fees or commissions are charged to enterprises or Digital Solution Providers (DSPs)" (KB §1). FAQ 3.3 says participation and all support features are "completely free of charge".
- **Policy alignment:** the "Digital Business" pillar of the [[digital-economy-society-policy-framework]] 2021–2035, and Pentagon 5 (Side 2) of the [[pentagonal-strategy-phase-1]] (KB §1; Brief §2.1).
- **Open to new businesses:** startups and newly established businesses "can fully participate or register" (FAQ 6.1).

## Priority activities (Program Book, 2023)

When asked **"What are EGD's priority activities?"**, always give the **five priority activities** from the EGD Program Book (`raw/DEBC/2023/…`, signed 21 Apr 2023, §3.3 and executive summary), which KB §1 and Brief §2.1 refer to:

1. **Digitalising enterprises' business operations and marketing** (General Business and Marketing Functions / business digitalisation). It has three parts:
   - a sector-based **digital readiness assessment**, an online questionnaire for micro, small, medium and large enterprises; this became the [[digital-adoption-assessment]];
   - a **Digital Solution Roadmap** in three growth stages: "Kickstart the Digital Journey", "Integrate in the Value Chain", "Strengthen Competitive Edge". These are the names later used for [[da2-kickstart-digital-journey]], [[da3-integrate-into-the-value-chain]] and [[da4-strengthen-competitive-edge]];
   - a **Digital Training Roadmap**.
2. **Sector research and planning** of enterprise digital transformation by sector.
3. **Developing the Enterprises Go Digital Platform (EGD Platform):** the "core" activity. It is a one-stop shop matching providers and enterprises by readiness level, with "Pre-Approved Solutions" from qualified local providers. See [[egd-platform]].
4. **Establishing an Enterprise Digital Transformation Center** (also called the "Enterprises Go Digital Hub"). It is a physical help desk for information, platform use, advice and training, with a head office in Phnom Penh and potential provincial expansion. §3.5 says expansion would start from 2025.
5. **A national dissemination and awareness campaign**, including an outreach strategy, platform content and success stories.

The book's answer to these activities is a response to **six challenges** enterprises face (executive summary):
1. knowledge and awareness of digital technology;
2. managing, maintaining and developing it;
3. hesitancy to use it;
4. public–private cooperation on platforms;
5. enterprise communication mechanisms;
6. lack of investment capital.

### The three core supports (FAQ)

FAQ 1.1 and 1.4 describe the enterprise-facing offer as three core activities:
1. [[da-training]], with "6 specialized training activities";
2. the [[da-grant]];
3. the [[egd-platform]].

> **Resolved** ([[source-conflicts]] #1). Both are right at different levels. The **five priority activities** are the programme's design (Program Book, 2023). The **three core supports** are what enterprises use today (FAQ, 2026). The DA courses and the grant voucher are not in the 2023 book; they came later.
> - Asked "what are EGD's priority activities?": give the **five priority activities** from the Program Book (business digitalisation; sector research; the EGD Platform; the Enterprise Digital Transformation Center; the awareness campaign).
> - Asked "what support can my business get?": give the **three core supports** (DA Training, DA Grant, EGD Platform).

### How the programme is run (Program Book §4.1, §3.5)

- **Leadership:** from January 2023 the programme is led and coordinated by the [[digital-economy-business-committee]], with its General Secretariat as technical staff, and implemented by [[techo-startup-center]].
- **Implementation plan:** runs 2022–2027 (Table 5).
- **Scope:** "Land and Expand for Efficiency". It starts with basic digital adoption in priority sectors, then moves to intermediate and advanced levels and all sectors and sizes, including startups.
- **Government strategy:** the [[pentagonal-strategy-phase-1]] (Aug 2023), Pentagon 5, Side 2, lists "Developing and launching the Enterprises Go Digital program" as a measure for the Seventh Legislature.

The platform's enterprise journey, from the KB and Brief:

```
DAA (≈5 min, free, no login required) → CamDigiKey login → DA Training (DA1–DA6) → DA Grant voucher → certified DSP solution
                                                         ↘ E-Learning (self-paced, 24/7)
```

Pages: [[camdigikey]], [[digital-adoption-assessment]], [[da-training]], [[e-learning]], [[da-grant]], [[digital-solution-providers]].

Beyond DA1–DA6, the platform also runs two separate training initiatives: **Digital Technology Capacity Training** (ពង្រឹងសមត្ថភាពឌីជីថលសម្រាប់សហគ្រិន) and the **Tourism Digital Adoption Initiative** (យុទ្ធនាការចាប់យកឌីជីថលសម្រាប់សហគ្រាសក្នុងវិស័យទេសចរណ៍). Both are separate from DA1–DA6, issue certificates on completion, and qualify participating enterprises to apply for the DA Grant. See [[digital-technology-capacity-training]] and [[tourism-digital-adoption-initiative]].

## Benefits for business owners (FAQ 3.1)

- **Knowledge and skills:** through [[e-learning]] and [[da-training]].
- **Digital Adoption Grants:** ease financial burdens and speed up adoption ([[da-grant]]).
- **Digital experts:** consulting, guidance and practical solutions ([[request-expert-consultation]]).
- **Partners and solutions:** the [[egd-platform]] as a gateway to suitable [[digital-solution-providers]].

## Who it serves

- **MSMEs**, formal and informal ("the main target of the EGD Program", Glossary 7.1).
- **Digital Solution Providers** ([[digital-solution-providers]]).
- **Development partners**.
- **Trainers and digital experts**.

These are the Brief's public user groups (§1.2), and the KB's "Key Users" table (§2) lists the same groups.
