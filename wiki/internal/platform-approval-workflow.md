Staff only. The five review stages an EGD Platform application passes through (Admin, Technical, Manager, Director, Finance), the possible decisions, and who has authority to approve, return or reject.

**Audience:** authorised EGD staff only (Brief §3.2: "This information is available only to authorised EGD staff"). Public users get only the outcome statuses and the public timelines; see [[da-grant-application]] and [[dsp-registration]].

## Approval stages (Brief §3.2, 2026-09-11)

All applications submitted on the EGD Platform go through "a structured multi-level review process".

| Stage | Name | Responsible person / team | Main task |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Admin Review | Coordinators / Admin staff | Check completeness of documents and basic information |
| 2 | Technical Review | Technical reviewers | Evaluate technical quality (mainly for DSP applications) |
| 3 | Manager Review | Department Manager | Mid-level approval for operational requests |
| 4 | Director Review | Director / Senior leadership | Final decision on strategic items, official DSP registration, and Grant Vouchers |
| 5 | Finance Review | Finance team | Validate documents and process grant disbursement |

```
Admin → Technical → Manager → Director → Finance (grant disbursement only)
```

## Decision outcomes (Brief §3.2; Glossary 7.6–7.8)

| Status | Khmer (Glossary) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Approved | បានអនុម័ត | Application is successful |
| Returned | ត្រូវបានត្រឡប់ | Information is incomplete or incorrect; can be corrected and resubmitted |
| Rejected | ត្រូវបានបដិសេធ | Application is denied and the decision is final |

## Authority summary (Brief §3.2)

- **Admin and Technical staff:** can pass or return applications.
- **Manager:** can approve operational requests or return them for correction.
- **Director / Senior leadership:** has final authority to approve or reject major requests, especially Grant Vouchers and DSP registration.
- **Finance:** handles only the payment and disbursement stage.

## How this fits the public timelines

- **Grant review time:** the public promise is a maximum of 2 working days for grant review (KB §5; GB ¶14). Stages 1–4 for a Grant Voucher must fit within that. The sources don't say how the time is split between stages.
- **Disbursement:** the Finance stage is the reimbursement step. The enterprise has already paid the DSP; Finance pays the enterprise after its progress reports (GB ¶17). See [[da-grant]].
- **Discretion:** the Guidebook's "TSC has discretion" (GB ¶14, ¶18) is exercised at Director level for vouchers.

**Detailed procedures** are in the official EGD Platform SOP (Brief §3.2), which is not in `raw/` ([[source-gaps]]). Don't describe stage-level steps, time limits per stage or named officers.

**Khmer:** the stage names have no Glossary terms; only the outcomes do (7.6–7.8).
