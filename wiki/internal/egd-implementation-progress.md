Staff only. The five areas the EGD Department tracks in its monthly progress reports, and what each covers. **No progress figures are in `raw/` yet.**

**Audience:** authorised EGD staff (Brief §1.2, §3.1).

The structure "follows the standard monthly reporting format used by the EGD Department" (Brief §3.1, 2026-09-11).

| Area | What it tracks (Brief §3.1.x) | Related page |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1.1 Digital Adoption Training activities | Courses conducted (DA1–DA6); participants (total and women); enterprises supported; locations (Phnom Penh and provinces); cumulative progress against annual targets | [[da-training]] |

## Data status

- **No figures available.** The progress data lives in the **EGD Program Progress Report 2025** (Brief A.1) and the monthly reports, none of which is in `raw/` ([[source-gaps]]). Until they are added, internal questions such as "how many vouchers were approved?" or "how many women trained?" get "I don't have that data", with a referral to the responsible staff.
- **Website counters aren't data.** On 2026-10-28 the DA programme and grant pages showed 0 registrants and 0 applications; these look like unloaded placeholders.

When a progress report is added, put the figures here with their reporting period and source, and keep superseded periods on the page.
