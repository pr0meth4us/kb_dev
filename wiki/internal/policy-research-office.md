Staff only. One of the two main offices of the EGD Department, responsible for research and policy recommendations on enterprise digital transformation. What the chatbot may say about it is pending a management decision.

**Audience:** authorised EGD staff. **Status:** pending management decision (Brief Part 4, 4.1; see [[pending-decisions]]).

**Khmer:** no official Glossary term ([[glossary]]).

## What the Brief states (§3.3, 2026-09-11)

- The Policy Research Office is **one of the two main offices** under the [[egd-department]]. The other office is not named.
- It is responsible for "research, data collection, analysis, and preparation of policy recommendations related to enterprise digital transformation".

## Possible content areas (to be confirmed by management)

1. Role and responsibilities of the Policy Research Office
2. Ongoing research projects
3. Policy recommendations and reports

## Rule until management decides (Brief §3.3)

- The chatbot **won't give detailed answers** about the Office's activities.
- Staff should **contact the responsible person directly** for research-related information. The person isn't named in `raw/`.
- The chatbot may state the two facts above (one of two main offices; its general remit). That matches option 1, "Only general role", and adds nothing beyond what the Brief itself states for staff.

When management decides 4.1, update this page and [[pending-decisions]], and log it.
