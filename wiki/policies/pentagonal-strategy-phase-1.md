The Royal Government's socio-economic policy agenda for the Seventh Legislature (August 2023). Its Pentagon 5, Side 2 explicitly commits to "developing and launching the Enterprises Go Digital program".

**Khmer:** no official Glossary term ([[glossary]]).

**Scope of this page.** The strategy is 95 pages. This page covers only the parts bearing on EGD. The filed English copy is a scan, OCR'd on 2026-10-28, and each page is headed "**Unofficial translation**"; the Khmer original prevails. The English copy was mirrored by Open Development Cambodia (24 Aug 2023), and the Khmer original is not filed.

## Pentagon 5: Development of Digital Economy and Society

- **Purpose:** the Government "considers digital technologies as a new important catalyst". It has launched the [[digital-economy-society-policy-framework]] 2021–2035 and the Cambodia Digital Government Policy 2022–2035.
- **Five sides (priorities):**
  1. building digital government and digital citizens;
  2. development of digital economy, digital business, e-commerce, and digital innovation system;
  3. building and development of digital infrastructures;
  4. trustworthiness building in digital system;
  5. development of financial technology.

## Side 2: Development of Digital Economy, Digital Business, e-Commerce, and Digital Innovation System (printed p. 73)

- **Strategic objectives:** "to promote digital transformation in the economic sectors; develop digital startups; and **promote the Enterprises Go Digital**, the development of digital value chains and e-commerce, and building and development of digital innovation system, to facilitate business and trade, aimed at accelerating economic growth."
- **Measures for the Seventh Legislature** (eight in all). The ones bearing on EGD:
  - (3) promoting digital startups, digital businesses and the e-commerce ecosystem, including "financing, and other supportive programs";
  - (4) "**Developing and launching the Enterprises Go Digital program.**"
  - (6) promoting entrepreneurship and innovation in production value chains.

## Link to EGD

- **KB §1 (2026-09-11):** EGD is part of "Pentagon 5 (Side 2) of the Pentagonal Strategy – Phase I of the Royal Government of Cambodia". The strategy confirms this: Side 2 names EGD in both its objective and measure 4.
- The [[egd-program]]'s 2023 Program Book predates the strategy by four months. It cites the Rectangular Strategy Phase IV, the predecessor of the Pentagonal Strategy.
- **Related, not EGD-specific:** Pentagon 3, Side 2, "Promotion of Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises…" (not read in detail).
