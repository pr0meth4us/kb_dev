The Royal Government's 15-year digital economy and society framework (May 2021). Its "Digital Businesses" pillar, which promotes digital adoption by enterprises and especially SMEs, is the part the EGD Program supports.

**Khmer:** no official Glossary term ([[glossary]]). The Khmer edition is filed alongside the English one.

**Scope of this page.** The framework is 180 pages (English). This page is a scoped reading of only the parts that bear on EGD, listed in `sources`. Other chapters (infrastructure, cybersecurity, digital government) are out of scope.

## The document

- **Title:** "Cambodia Digital Economy and Society Policy Framework 2021 – 2035", Royal Government of Cambodia. "Prepared by the Supreme National Economic Council", May 2021 (cover).
- **Vision (cover):** "Building a vibrant digital economy and society by laying the foundations to promote digital adoption and transformation in all social actors including the state, citizens, and businesses, to accelerate new economic growth and promote social welfare in the new normal."
- **Editions:**
  - English, 180 pp, with a clean text layer; downloaded from the Digital Economy and Business Committee's publications page, digitaleconomy.gov.kh, on 2026-10-28.
  - Khmer, 164 pp; filed but not transcribed.

## The "Digital Businesses" pillar

- **Structure (Table 1):** the framework's strategic priorities are grouped into foundations and pillars. **Digital Businesses** is a pillar with three strategic priorities:
  1. Enterprise Digital Transformation;
  2. Entrepreneurship and Startup Ecosystems;
  3. Digital Value Chains.
- **Fifth of the five goals:** "Enabling digital businesses focuses on encouraging enterprises as a whole and especially small and medium enterprises to adopt digital technologies widely, the creation of an ecosystem for startups, and the participation in the regional and global digital chains".
- **Situation assessment (§2.5, Digital Businesses):**
  - Enterprise technology use is "growing rapidly but is still limited", mostly websites and social media. "Digital systems for business management and automated data collection and analysis tools" are not yet widely adopted.
  - The Government's support mechanisms include "the Skills Development Fund, Entrepreneurship Development Fund, the "Techo" Startup Center, and the SME Bank, aiming at providing skill, technical and financial support". These are the bodies behind EGD: see [[skills-development-fund]] and [[techo-startup-center]].
- **Policy measures (§3.4.5 A, "Digital Enterprise Transformation")** that match what EGD does:
  - "Establish mechanisms for disseminating, consulting, and promoting better understanding on digital economy, with the ability to provide services free of charge or with acceptable fees, to enterprises with the intention to adopt digital technologies". Compare EGD's free [[digital-adoption-assessment]], [[da-training]] and [[request-expert-consultation]].
  - "Develop relevant infrastructures and digital platforms, to support enterprises", including platforms for "accounting and financial management and customer relations" and for market expansion. Compare the [[egd-platform]].
  - "Promote collaboration between public and private sectors … to provide enterprises with digital services and solutions on business operations, including staff management, accounting management, order management, inventory management, and customer management". Compare [[digital-solution-providers]] and the eligible categories of the [[da-grant]].
  - "Promote adoption and use of digital payments".
  - Improve financial, technical and capacity support by identifying priority sectors for digital adoption, establishing centres to support enterprises, and running training programmes.

## Link to EGD

- **KB §1; Brief §2.1 (2026-09-11):** the [[egd-program]] is part of "the 'Digital Business' pillar" of this framework.
- **Naming:** the framework itself calls the pillar "Digital Businesses" (plural). The chatbot documents' "Digital Business" refers to the same pillar.
- **What the framework doesn't say:** it predates EGD by name. It sets out measures that EGD later implements, but it doesn't mention the "Enterprises Go Digital" programme. That link is stated in the EGD documents, not here.
