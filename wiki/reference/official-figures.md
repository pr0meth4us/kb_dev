## Notes

- **Riel grouping:** the English sources write "1,600,000 Riels" with commas. The Glossary's Khmer writes ១.៦០០.០០០ with full stops, which is Khmer convention. Quote each language's form as written.
- **Voucher validity start:** only the Glossary (5.5) says validity runs "from the date of issuance". The KB and Brief say only "valid for up to 12 months". Both are compatible.
- **"Informal" grant label:** the KB and Brief label the $250 row "Informal Economy (Unregistered / Micro)". The Glossary (5.4) says "unregistered/informal enterprises", and the Guidebook (¶9) defines the formal track by legal registration. So the wiki takes the tier to follow registration, not size: a registered micro enterprise is formal ($400). This is an inference; see [[da-grant]] and [[source-gaps]].
- **Guidebook amounts:** the 2024 Guidebook (¶11–12) has a single maximum for everyone, 400 US dollars or ១ ៦០០ ០០០ riels per voucher, with no $250 tier. The 2026 two-tier figures above take precedence ([[source-conflicts]] #7). The Guidebook writes riels with spaces (១ ៦០០ ០០០); the Glossary uses full stops (១.៦០០.០០០). In Khmer answers, use the Glossary form.
- The DA Grant, DA Training, DAA and DSP rows were checked against all three sources in batch 1 but are ingested in full in batches 2–5. Their pages may not exist yet.
