## Notes

- **Riel grouping:** the English sources write "1,600,000 Riels" with commas. The Glossary's Khmer writes ១.៦០០.០០០ with full stops, which is Khmer convention. Quote each language's form as written.
- **Enterprise size & grant tier:** Micro, small and medium size is **not** considered. Registered status ($400 formal vs $250 informal) is regardless of size and depends strictly on the registration documents the enterprise holds.
- **Reimbursement currency & method:** All reimbursement transactions are in **Khmer Riel (KHR) only** via **KHQR** (no US dollar payments). EGD reviews and approves reimbursement requests in **7 to 10 working days**.
- **DSP requirements & documents:** The 2024 Guidebook and older KB §6 lists of DSP requirements and documents (including the 14 June 2020 Enterprise Announcement cut-off) **do not apply in practice** and must **not** be listed by the chatbot; refer DSPs to the EGD team via [[contacts]].
