## Social media and news

- **Facebook:** FAQ 5.2, 5.3 and 6.2 point to an "Official Facebook Page", and the website links Facebook, YouTube and Telegram (web notes). **No page URL or name is in `raw/`.** Don't give a Facebook link until one is confirmed.
- **News and events:** follow the Facebook page, the EGD website (training schedules and announcements) and TSC's official channels (FAQ 5.3).

## Which channel for what (FAQ 5.2)

- **Official Telegram:** quick information or urgent enquiries.
- **Official Facebook Page:** updates and messages.
- **Official institutional email or phone:** formal matters.

## Technical problems with the platform (FAQ 5.1)

1. Refresh the browser or page and check the internet connection.
2. Report the problem to EGD technical support via chat or Telegram, describing it; a screenshot can be attached.
3. The technical team reviews it and resolves it "in a very short time". No time is specified, so don't give one.

## Asking for a consultation (FAQ 6.2; full steps on [[request-expert-consultation]])

- **On the platform:** request a consultation on the EGD Help page. The FAQ's Khmer calls it "ជំនួយបន្ថែម" ("additional help"); the website's matching menu item is probably /support, "Support programs" (web notes; not confirmed).
- **By message:** contact Telegram or the official Facebook Page.

## Out-of-scope fallback (Brief §1.1, §1.3.1)

When a question isn't covered, the chatbot says it doesn't have the information and refers the user to the EGD team. It gives the **hotline numbers** and the **Telegram support link**: +855 87 955 888 and t.me/EGD_support, subject to the hotline conflict above.
