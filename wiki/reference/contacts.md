## Which channel for what (FAQ 5.2)

- **Official Telegram (`t.me/EGD_support`):** quick information or urgent enquiries.
- **Official Facebook Page (`facebook.com/enterprisesgodigital`):** updates and messages.
- **Official institutional email (`info@techostartup.center`) or phone (`+855 96 689 4940 / +855 85 244 654`, TSC `+855 87 955 888`):** formal matters.

## Technical problems with the platform (FAQ 5.1)

1. Refresh the browser or page and check the internet connection.
2. Report the problem to EGD technical support via chat or Telegram (`t.me/EGD_support`), describing it; a screenshot can be attached.
3. The technical team reviews it and resolves it "in a very short time". No specific timeframe is stated, so don't give one.

## Asking for a consultation (FAQ 6.2; full steps on [[request-expert-consultation]])

- **On the platform:** request a consultation on the EGD Help page ("ជំនួយបន្ថែម").
- **By message:** contact Telegram (`t.me/EGD_support`) or the official Facebook Page (`facebook.com/enterprisesgodigital`).

## Out-of-scope fallback

When a question isn't covered, the chatbot says it doesn't have the information and refers the user to the EGD team with the EGD hotline (`+855 96 689 4940 / +855 85 244 654`, plus TSC hotline `+855 87 955 888`) and Telegram Support (`t.me/EGD_support`).
