Places where the sources disagreed and how each one was settled. All previously open conflicts were resolved by the 2026-11-08 KB Additions (`raw/EGD/2026/2026-11-08_KnowledgeBase_EGD-Chatbot-Knowledge-Base-Additions.md`).

| # | Topic | Disagreement | Resolution | Settled by | Status | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Programme activities | KB §1 and Brief §2.1: "five priority activities", none listed. FAQ 1.1 and 1.4: "three core activities" (DA Training, DA Grant, EGD Platform). | Both are right at different levels. The Program Book (§3.3, 2023) lists the five priority activities (programme design); the FAQ's three are the enterprise-facing supports. Give whichever matches the question. | EGD Program Book (21 Apr 2023) | resolved | [[egd-program]] |
| 2 | Hotline numbers | Brief §2.1: three numbers. KB §7.1 and website: `+855 87 955 888` only. | The **EGD hotline** is `+855 96 689 4940 / +855 85 244 654`. `+855 87 955 888` is the **Techo Startup Center (TSC) hotline** (still active; give alongside the EGD hotline for TSC questions in general). | KB §7.1 Additions (2026-11-08) | resolved | [[contacts]] |
| 3 | DAA availability & login | KB §3 and Brief §2.3: DAA available after CamDigiKey login at `/digital-readiness-assessment`. Live platform (Nov 2026): URL is `/ecosystem/enterprise/digital-adoption-assessment` and opens without login. | DAA is live at `enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/enterprise/digital-adoption-assessment` and **requires no CamDigiKey or platform login**. | KB §3 Additions (2026-11-08) | resolved | [[digital-adoption-assessment]] |
| 4 | Eligible grant solutions | KB §5: 11 categories. Brief §2.5: 8 categories. Live directory: 8 categories (including "Other"). | **Every digital solution reviewed, approved, and actively listed in the official EGD Platform directory is eligible for DA Grant voucher support**, including solutions under "Other". | KB §6 Additions (2026-11-08) | resolved | [[da-grant]], [[digital-solution-providers]] |
| 5 | Other training programmes | KB and Brief: DA1–DA6 only. Glossary 4.9 and 4.10, and the website: also Digital Technology Capacity Training and the Tourism Digital Adoption Initiative. | DA Training has 6 activities (DA1–DA6). Digital Technology Capacity Training and the Tourism Digital Adoption Initiative are **separate initiatives** (not DA1–DA6), **and both give a certificate on completion and qualify participating enterprises to apply for the DA Grant**. | KB §4 Additions (2026-11-08) | resolved | [[da-training]] |
| 6 | DSP page URL & requirements | KB §6 URL truncated; KB §6 and Guidebook list DSP requirements and 5 required documents. | URL is `enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/service-provider` (solution directory: `/ecosystem/service-provider/solution`). **Do not list DSP or Digital Solution requirements or documents** (the Guidebook and KB §6 lists don't apply in practice); refer DSPs to EGD contacts. | KB §6 Additions (2026-11-08) | resolved | [[digital-solution-providers]], [[dsp-registration]] |
| 7 | Grant amount tiers & Guidebook divergence | KB §5 (2026): $400 formal and $250 informal. Guidebook ¶11–12 (Nov 2024): a single maximum of $400. | There is no new edition of the 2024 Guidebook, but practical implementation has diverged significantly. Use the 2026 two-tier figures ($400 formal / $250 informal); enterprise size (micro/small/medium) is not considered — tier follows registration documents. | KB §5 Additions (2026-11-08) | resolved | [[da-grant]] |
| 8 | "No direct cash" vs reimbursement | FAQ 6.3: no direct cash handouts. KB §5 and GB ¶17: TSC reimburses the enterprise. | Both hold: no upfront cash; TSC reimburses spending made after voucher issuance, in Khmer Riel (KHR) only via KHQR, within 7 to 10 working days of the reimbursement request. | Guidebook ¶17; KB §5 Additions | resolved | [[da-grant]] |
| 9 | Is DA Training required for a grant? | KB §4: grant eligibility is a training *benefit*. GB ¶6: beneficiaries "must participate" in a DA course, but non-participants are supported case by case. | Training is the expected route and simplifies the application; it isn't an absolute bar. | Guidebook ¶6, ¶13 | resolved | [[da-grant]] |
