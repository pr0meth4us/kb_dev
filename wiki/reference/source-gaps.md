## Resolved by the 2026-11-08 KB Additions (`2026-11-08_KnowledgeBase_EGD-Chatbot-Knowledge-Base-Additions.md`)

The following previously open gaps were settled on 2026-11-08:
- **EGD Department's offices (KB §1 Additions):** The public duties of both offices — **Policy Research Office** (ការិយាល័យស្រាវជ្រាវគោលនយោបាយ, Article 15) and **Digital Capture Office** (ការិយាល័យការចាប់យកឌីជីថល, Article 14, which also handles the DA Grant, DSP registration and the platform) — are now documented from the Prakas on TSC Offices 2024 ([[egd-department]]).
- **Programme funding & ADB MSME project (`TA 10365-CAM`) (KB §1 Additions):** The **Skills Development Fund (SDF)** finances the core **EGD Program** under MEF, and **does not fund the ADB project**. The ADB project (**TA 10365-CAM** / Project Number **55255-004**, Contract `S206710`, 2024–2028) is an independent ADB technical assistance project funded by the **Government of Japan** via the **Japan Special Fund (JSF)** (**USD $1.35 million**), implementing the 3-campaign Digital Adoption Initiative (starting with Campaign 1 on Tourism MSMEs) with targets of **1,000 open call applications**, **500 digital diagnostics completed** (1,200 with pilots), **500 Digital Adoption Plans prepared (≥250 implemented)**, **6 sector association partnerships**, and **8+ events per campaign** (≥35% women's participation) ([[skills-development-fund]], [[adb-msme-digital-adoption-project]], [[tourism-digital-adoption-initiative]]).
- **DAA structure, login, URL, and levels (KB §3 Additions):** Official URL (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/enterprise/digital-adoption-assessment`), official Khmer name (`ការវាយតម្លៃកម្រិតនៃការចាប់យកឌីជីថល (DAA)`), **no login required**, 4 sections / 24 scored questions (+ up to 7 follow-ups), 4 result levels (Non-Digital, Basic, Intermediate, Advanced), PDF download + Telegram/email delivery, result key for reopening/retaking, and Khmer/English language toggle ([[digital-adoption-assessment]]).
- **DA Training logistics, Certificates & E-Learning (KB §4 Additions):**
  - Course duration (hours/days), delivery format (in-person vs. online), detailed syllabus, and venues **all vary per course and per cohort** and are posted on each cohort's page when registration opens ([[da-training]]).
  - Both DA Training certificates and E-Learning completion certificates (`https://lms.enterprisedigital.gov.kh/`) are issued and can be verified online at **`verify.gov.kh`**, and **completing E-Learning alone satisfies the training expectation for the DA Grant** (`[[e-learning]]` course catalogue is continuously updated as ongoing improvement) ([[da-training]], [[e-learning]]).
- **Trainers & other programmes (KB §4 Additions):** How trainers are selected (registered DSPs and subject matter experts applying at `/directory/trainers/registration` via CamDigiKey login, profile at `/user/profile/info`), polite refusal on trainer pay ([[trainers-and-digital-experts]]), and confirmation that **Digital Technology Capacity Training** and the **Tourism Digital Adoption Initiative** are separate from DA1–DA6, issue certificates, and qualify participants for the DA Grant ([[digital-technology-capacity-training]], [[tourism-digital-adoption-initiative]]).
- **DA Grant rules, reimbursement & progress-report form (KB §5 Additions):** No fixed deadline; no retroactive claims for software bought before voucher issuance; multiple businesses per owner allowed; **expired or revoked vouchers CAN be re-applied for**; `"other conditions apply"` (GB ¶9) is a standard administrative flexibility clause (not a gap); formal documents (Certificate of Incorporation or One-Window licence + proof of at least 6 months of operation); enterprise size (micro/small/medium) not considered; 4-step online reimbursement/progress-report form (`ការស្នើសុំទូទាត់ជំនួយថវិកាគាំទ្រ` — Step 1 Declaration, Step 2 Efficiency/Results/Feedback + Receipt/Invoice, Purchase Date ≥3 months before application, and Proof of Payment showing recipient/date/amount/successful status, Step 3 Bank Account, Step 4 Review); reimbursement in **Khmer Riel (KHR) only** via **KHQR**; reimbursement approval in **7 to 10 working days** ([[da-grant]], [[da-grant-application]]).
- **DSPs & Solution Directory (KB §6 Additions):** No fixed timeframe for DSP approval; **do not list DSP or Digital Solution requirements or documents** (refer to EGD contacts); **DSP status does not expire** unless the DSP requests to be removed; **foreign companies may register as DSPs** if they have operations or a licensed registration in Cambodia; DSPs log in via CamDigiKey (`/user/profile/info`); all 42 listed solutions at `enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/service-provider/solution` are grant-eligible with package prices in KHR ([[digital-solution-providers]], [[dsp-registration]]).
- **Contacts & CamDigiKey (KB §7.1 Additions):** EGD hotline (`+855 96 689 4940 / +855 85 244 654`), TSC hotline (`+855 87 955 888`), Telegram Group (`t.me/enterprisesGoDigitalgroup`) and Hub (`t.me/+TpLqvipzTQQzMDVk`), Facebook (`facebook.com/enterprisesgodigital`), YouTube (`youtube.com/@enterprisesgodigital`), and CamDigiKey app download / guide (`https://camdigikey.gov.kh/`) ([[contacts]], [[camdigikey]]).

## Policy-mandated refusals & items to refer to EGD contacts

When asked about any of the following, the chatbot must follow the specified refusal/referral rule and give the contacts in [[contacts]]:

- **Trainer payment:** politely decline to answer; do not give any figure ([[trainers-and-digital-experts]]).
- **DSP & Digital Solution requirements and documents:** do not list them (the Guidebook and old KB §6 lists do not apply in practice); refer the DSP to [[contacts]] ([[digital-solution-providers]], [[dsp-registration]]).
- **DSP approval timeframe:** no fixed timeframe; refer the DSP to [[contacts]] ([[dsp-registration]]).
- **Internal staff-only documents:** out of scope for public users (EGD Program Progress Report 2025 monthly figures, Internal FAQ, internal staff SOP workflows).
- **Response times for technical support or consultations:** no specific timeframe is stated beyond "a very short time" (FAQ 5.1; [[contacts]], [[request-expert-consultation]]).
