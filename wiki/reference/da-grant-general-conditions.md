The eight General Conditions that every business owner and DSP accepts by applying for, or providing a solution under, the DA Grant. They cover TSC's limited liability, data ownership, conflicts of interest and disputes.

**Source:** Guidebook Annex 1, "លក្ខខណ្ឌទូទៅនៃការទទួលយកជំនួយថវិកាគាំទ្រការធ្វើបរិវត្តកម្មឌីជីថលសហគ្រាស" (General Conditions for Receiving the Enterprise Digital Transformation Support Grant). This is the Nov 2024 Guidebook, version 6; `date` is the version date in the PDF title (2024-11-08). Below is an English summary of the Khmer; Khmer prevails (condition 7).

- **Who is bound:** the business owner and the DSP ("the parties"), with Techo Startup Center ([[techo-startup-center]]). The conditions are part of the Guidebook. Applying for or providing a solution under the grant means the parties have read, understood, accepted and agreed to follow them. GB ¶13 says the same for the applicant at submission.

## The eight conditions

1. **Quality and professionalism:** the DSP provides a quality, professional solution on the timeline and terms agreed between the parties. The business uses it as agreed. Specific terms go in a **contract between the business and the DSP**. **TSC isn't responsible for the quality or delivery of the solution.**
2. **Full cooperation with TSC:** the parties take part in TSC's monitoring and evaluation when asked, and carry out the roles set in the Guidebook.
3. **Confidentiality and data ownership:**
   - The parties share all data used in giving and receiving the solution with TSC, so it can study the voucher's effectiveness.
   - Neither party may disclose the other's confidential information to third parties other than TSC.
   - **All data used for the solution belongs to the business owner.** The DSP may not use the business's data without the owner's consent and permission, except as required by law. A DSP that does so is liable under Cambodian law.
4. **Conflict of interest:** a party with a conflict of interest (financial, family or personal) must notify the other party and TSC promptly.
5. **Limited liability:** TSC acts as **facilitator**. Its only obligation is to **pay (reimburse)** the business owner for the solution once the owner has met all duties in the Guidebook. TSC isn't liable for any loss, damage or dispute during or after the service.
6. **Disputes:** disputes from breaching these conditions or the business–DSP contract are settled first by negotiation or conciliation, then under Cambodian law. **TSC doesn't decide disputes between the parties**; competent institutions do.
7. **Language:** the conditions are made in Khmer. If a translation conflicts, the Khmer text prevails.
8. **Miscellaneous:** the parties follow the Guidebook, these General Conditions and the specific conditions of the Grant Voucher.

## Related obligations elsewhere in the Guidebook

- **Both parties** (GB ¶19):
  - use the voucher fully and as the Guidebook sets out;
  - cooperate with TSC's monitoring of effectiveness and impact;
  - agree to share data;
  - help resolve issues with the voucher's use or payment;
  - bear their own platform-connection, IT, equipment and staffing costs;
  - obey Cambodian law.
- **TSC's roles** (GB ¶18):
  - accept or reject applications at its discretion;
  - give owners information and training;
  - list the platform's solutions **without pushing any particular DSP or solution**; owners are encouraged to choose DSPs that meet digital-technology standards and the law;
  - share voucher and beneficiary data **after beneficiaries consent**;
  - update the Guidebook;
  - carry out duties set by the MEF, its technical and financial supervisor, and the donor's conditions.
