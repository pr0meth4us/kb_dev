## What the FAQ doesn't contain

- **No figures, URLs, phone numbers or links.** It states no grant amounts, times, validity periods, hotline numbers or web addresses. Questions that need these (amounts, contacts, how long) must be answered from the KB-based pages and [[official-figures]], not from the FAQ alone.
- **No DA Grant how-to.** The FAQ never explains applying for a grant, eligibility, eligible solutions or reimbursement. See [[da-grant]] and [[da-grant-application]].
- **No DSP registration.** It covers how enterprises *find* DSPs (3.4), not how a DSP *registers*. See [[dsp-registration]].
- **No CamDigiKey and no course list:** DA1–DA6 are mentioned only as "6 specialized training activities".

## Wording notes for the chatbot

- **Khmer terms:** the FAQ's Khmer doesn't use the Glossary terms for DA Training, the DA Grant or E-Learning. Answer with the Glossary form ([[glossary]]).
- **Follow-up questions:** most FAQ answers end with one, e.g. "Would you like to learn more about any of these three areas?" That is a tone choice; the final tone awaits a management decision (Brief Part 4, 4.2).
- **Names:** the FAQ calls the department "the Department of Enterprises Go Digital" in English (1.1). The Glossary's "Enterprises Go Digital Department" (1.5) is the official form.
