Step by step: apply online on the EGD Platform, TSC reviews within 2 working days, you receive the voucher, pay the DSP, report progress, and TSC reimburses you.

## Before you apply

- Log in to the [[egd-platform]] with [[camdigikey]].
- Recommended: take the [[digital-adoption-assessment]], then a [[da-training]] course. If you're already registered for a DA course, the grant application asks only for extra information (GB ¶13). See [[da-grant]] on whether training is required.
- Check you meet the conditions on [[da-grant]]: formal enterprises registered and operating at least 6 months; informal businesses with proof of operation.
- Read the **General Conditions** ([[da-grant-general-conditions]]). Submitting means you accept them (GB ¶13).

## Steps

```
Apply online → TSC review (max 2 working days) → voucher issued (valid up to 12 months)
   → pay DSP & start using → progress reports (from 3 months) → TSC reimburses
```

**KB §5 (2026-09-11):**

1. Click "Apply for Digital Adoption Grant" on the EGD Platform.
2. Fill in business information and upload the required documents.
3. Select the preferred digital solution.
4. State the reason for choosing the solution.
5. Submit the application (processing time: maximum 2 working days).
6. Receive the voucher (valid for up to 12 months).

The website (2026-10-28) shows the same six steps. Its button reads "ដាក់ពាក្យស្នើសុំជំនួយថវិកាគាំទ្រ".

### What happens at each stage (Guidebook ¶13–17)

- **Step 1, application (¶13):**
  - Apply online on the EGD Platform, attaching the documents TSC needs to assess you.
  - Already registered for an EGD DA course on the platform: you only add the extra information or attachments.
  - Not registered for a course: you complete all the information in the DA course registration guide, plus the grant information.
  - Documents must be complete, correct and **not falsified**.
  - Choose a solution suited to your business from a DSP on the EGD Platform.
- **Step 2, review (¶14):** within **at most 2 working days** of receipt, TSC assesses the application against the Guidebook's conditions. The outcome is one of three:
  - **Approved:** the application is correct.
  - **Returned:** information is incomplete or attachments are wrong. TSC gives clear reasons so you can correct and resubmit.
  - **Rejected:** the application doesn't meet the conditions, laws or regulations.

  If needed, TSC calls a **technical meeting** (in person or online) with you and the DSP before approving. TSC has discretion based on your qualifications and actual situation.
- **Step 3, voucher issued (¶15):** you get an **official notification** of the voucher from TSC. Valid for up to 12 months from issuance; it expires automatically if not used (¶12).
- **Step 4, implementation (¶16):** start using the solution after officially receiving the voucher. Submit progress reports showing its impact or outputs.
- **Step 5, payment (¶17):** **you pay the DSP first.** TSC **reimburses** you after receiving your progress reports, by stage of actual implementation.
  - **Stopping early:** to stop using the solution before the voucher period ends, notify TSC with reasons and wait for its assessment to close the payment.

The three outcomes match the Glossary statuses Approved (បានអនុម័ត), Returned (ត្រូវបានត្រឡប់) and Rejected (ត្រូវបានបដិសេធ) (Glossary 7.6–7.8).

## After reimbursement

Progress reports continue **in stages from 3 months onward**, on measurable results (sales, productivity, costs). You also cooperate in surveys (GB ¶20). See [[da-grant]].

## Documents

- **Formal enterprises:** the KB says only "upload required documents". GB ¶9 implies proof of legal registration and at least 6 months of operation, but **no list is given**.
- **Informal businesses:** GB ¶9 gives examples of acceptable proof: an informal-economy recognition certificate, a permit, or any certificate from the sub-national one-window office.

For anything more specific, refer to [[contacts]]; see [[source-gaps]].

**Not in the sources:**
- how long TSC takes to reimburse after a progress report;
- the progress-report format;
- how the reimbursement is paid (bank transfer or otherwise).
