Step by step: apply online on the EGD Platform, TSC reviews within a maximum of 2 working days, you receive the voucher (valid up to 12 months), buy and pay the DSP after voucher issuance, submit your reimbursement request and progress reports, and EGD reviews and approves reimbursement in 7 to 10 working days, paying in Khmer Riel (KHR) only via KHQR.

## Before you apply

- Log in to the [[egd-platform]] with [[camdigikey]].
- **No fixed application deadline:** there is no fixed deadline to apply; vouchers depend on available funding (GB ¶11).
- **Do not buy the software before getting the voucher:** solutions bought **before** the voucher is issued cannot be claimed. Only solutions bought **after** the voucher is issued can be claimed.
- **Recommended first steps:** take the [[digital-adoption-assessment]], then a [[da-training]] course (or [[digital-technology-capacity-training]] / [[tourism-digital-adoption-initiative]]). If you're already registered for a DA course, the grant application asks only for extra information (GB ¶13). See [[da-grant]] on whether training is required.
- **Check eligibility and required documents:**
  - **Registered (formal) enterprises ($400 / 1,600,000 Riels):** must hold a **Certificate of Incorporation or One-Window licence** AND **proof of at least 6 (six) months of business operation** as of the application date (a Digital Adoption Plan is encouraged). Enterprise size (micro, small, medium) is not considered. If registered less than 6 months ago, the business must wait until it has 6 months of operation to apply for the formal grant (GB ¶9).
  - **Unregistered (informal) businesses ($250 / 1,000,000 Riels):** do **not** need business registration or 6 months of formal registration; they need a clear purpose for using the voucher and proof of actual business operation (e.g. an informal-economy recognition certificate, a permit, or a sub-national one-window certificate) (GB ¶9).
- Read the **General Conditions** ([[da-grant-general-conditions]]). Submitting means you accept them (GB ¶13).

## Steps

```
Apply online → TSC review (max 2 working days) → voucher issued (valid up to 12 months)
   → buy & pay DSP after voucher issued → submit reimbursement request / progress reports
   → EGD approves reimbursement in 7–10 working days → Finance transfers KHR via KHQR
```

**KB §5 (2026-09-11):**

1. Click "Apply for Digital Adoption Grant" on the EGD Platform (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh/digital-adoption-grant`).
2. Fill in business information and upload the required documents.
3. Select the preferred digital solution from the official directory (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/service-provider/solution`).
4. State the reason for choosing the solution.
5. Submit the application (processing time: maximum 2 working days).
6. Receive the voucher (valid for up to 12 months).

The website (2026-10-28) shows the same six steps. Its button reads "ដាក់ពាក្យស្នើសុំជំនួយថវិកាគាំទ្រ".

### What happens at each stage (Guidebook ¶13–17)

- **Step 1, application:**
  - Apply online on the EGD Platform, attaching the required documents.
  - Already registered for an EGD DA course on the platform: you only add the extra information or attachments.
  - Not registered for a course: you complete all the information in the DA course registration guide, plus the grant information.
  - Documents must be complete, correct, and not falsified.
  - Choose an approved digital solution from a DSP on the EGD Platform. Every digital solution reviewed, approved, and actively listed in the official platform directory is eligible.
- **Step 2, application review:** within **at most 2 working days** of receipt, TSC assesses the application. The outcome is one of three (Glossary 7.6–7.8):
  - **Approved (បានអនុម័ត):** the application is correct.
  - **Returned (ត្រូវបានត្រឡប់):** information is incomplete or attachments are wrong. TSC gives clear reasons so you can correct and resubmit.
  - **Rejected (ត្រូវបានបដិសេធ):** the application doesn't meet the conditions, laws, or regulations (decision is final).

  If needed, TSC calls a **technical meeting** (in person or online) with you and the DSP before approving.
- **Step 3, voucher issued:** you get an **official notification** of the voucher from TSC. Valid for up to 12 months from issuance; it expires automatically if not used (GB ¶12). If a voucher expires or is revoked, the enterprise **can re-apply**.
- **Step 4, purchase, implementation, and online reimbursement request:**
  - Purchase and start using the solution **after** officially receiving the voucher (solutions bought before voucher issuance cannot be claimed). **You pay the DSP first.**
  - After using the solution (purchase date must be **at least 3 months prior to the request date**), submit the 4-step online **Grant Reimbursement Request** on the EGD Platform:
    1. **Step 1 — Declaration:** Confirm compliance with grant terms.
    2. **Step 2 — Experience Using the Digital Solution:** Provide qualitative feedback across 3 areas (Efficiency, Results, and Feedback) and attach 3 required proofs:
       - Receipt or purchase invoice for the digital solution;
       - Date of purchase (must be at least 3 months before the reimbursement application date);
       - Proof of payment showing recipient name, date, amount, and successful transaction status.
    3. **Step 3 — Bank Account Information:** Enter bank details for KHR transfer via KHQR.
    4. **Step 4 — Review Your Application:** Review and submit.
- **Step 5, reimbursement approval and payment:**
  - **Reimbursement review time:** EGD reviews the reimbursement request and gives approval in **7 to 10 working days**.
  - **Payment method:** Money transfer by the Finance team to the enterprise via **KHQR**.
  - **Payment currency:** **All transactions are in Khmer Riel (KHR) only.** EGD does not make payments in US dollars.
  - **Stopping early:** if you want to stop using the solution before the voucher period ends, notify TSC with clear reasons and wait for TSC's evaluation to close the payment (GB ¶17).

## Multiple vouchers and multiple businesses

- **One business, multiple solutions:** One business may apply for more than one voucher for **different digital solutions**, provided the **combined value does not exceed the maximum** ($400 formal / $250 informal).
- **One owner, multiple separate businesses:** An owner who has two or more separate businesses or shops (for example, a café and a salon) **may apply separately for each business**, if each business meets the eligibility conditions.

## After reimbursement

Progress reports continue **in stages from 3 months onward**, on measurable results (sales, productivity, costs). You also cooperate in surveys (GB ¶20). See [[da-grant]].

## Required documents summary

- **Registered (formal) enterprises:**
  - **Certificate of Incorporation** or **One-Window licence**
  - **Proof of at least 6 (six) months of business operation** as of the application date
  - **Digital Adoption Plan** (encouraged)
- **Unregistered (informal) businesses:**
  - Clear statement of purpose for using the voucher
  - Supporting documents proving the business operates (e.g. informal-economy recognition certificate, permit, or certificate from the sub-national one-window office)

For any questions on specific documents, contact the EGD team via [[contacts]].
