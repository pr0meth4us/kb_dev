A business owner's first steps with the EGD Program: visit the platform, take the free 5-minute Digital Adoption Assessment (DAA) (no login needed), then log in with CamDigiKey to join training, E-Learning, expert consultation, and apply for the DA Grant.

## Steps

1. **Visit the EGD Platform (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh`):** explore digital content on using digital solutions and discover the support programmes. See [[egd-platform]].
2. **Take the Digital Adoption Assessment (DAA) (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/enterprise/digital-adoption-assessment`):** learn the enterprise's Business Health, Digital Maturity Level and Digital Readiness Level in approximately 5 minutes for free. **No CamDigiKey or platform login is required to take the DAA.** The assessment is the recommended first step before applying for Training or Grants. See [[digital-adoption-assessment]].
3. **Log in with [[camdigikey]] and seek support:** register for [[e-learning]] (24/7, with a certificate verifiable at `verify.gov.kh`), apply to join [[da-training]] (DA1–DA6, or the separate initiatives [[digital-technology-capacity-training]] and [[tourism-digital-adoption-initiative]]), or get advice from digital experts ([[request-expert-consultation]]).
4. **Apply for the [[da-grant]] and adopt a certified digital solution:** apply for a Grant Voucher (up to $400 / 1,600,000 Riels for formal registered enterprises operating at least 6 months, or up to $250 / 1,000,000 Riels for informal unregistered businesses) to buy an approved solution from the [[digital-solution-providers]] directory (`enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/service-provider/solution`).

```
Visit platform → DAA (≈5 min, free, no login required) → CamDigiKey login → E-Learning / DA Training / expert consultation → DA Grant → DSP solution
```

## Good to know

- **It's free:** participation and every support feature cost nothing (FAQ 3.3; KB §1).
- **New businesses are welcome:** startups and newly established businesses "can fully participate or register" (FAQ 6.1), though the formal DA Grant ($400) requires at least 6 months of business operation as of the application date (GB ¶9).
- **Both formal and informal businesses** can take the DAA, join training, and apply for the DA Grant (KB §3–5).
