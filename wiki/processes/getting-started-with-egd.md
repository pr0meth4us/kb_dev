A business owner's first steps with the EGD Program: visit the platform, take the DAA, then seek support through training, E-Learning or expert consultation.

## Steps (FAQ 3.2, 2026-09-11)

1. **Visit the EGD Platform:** explore digital content on using digital solutions, and discover the support programmes. See [[egd-platform]]. Logging in needs a [[camdigikey]] account (KB §2).
2. **Take the assessment (DAA):** learn the enterprise's current status and where it needs to develop. See [[digital-adoption-assessment]]. The KB (§3) calls the DAA "the first recommended step before applying for Training or Grants".
3. **Seek support:** register for [[e-learning]], apply to join [[da-training]], or get advice from digital experts ([[request-expert-consultation]]).

```
Visit platform (CamDigiKey login) → DAA (≈5 min, free) → E-Learning / DA Training / expert consultation → DA Grant → DSP solution
```

The last two steps, the [[da-grant]] and a [[digital-solution-providers]] solution, come from KB §2's list of main functions and from the website's enterprise steps. FAQ 3.2 stops at "seek support".

## Good to know

- **It's free:** participation and every support feature cost nothing (FAQ 3.3; KB §1).
- **New businesses are welcome:** startups and newly established businesses "can fully participate or register" (FAQ 6.1).
- **Both formal and informal businesses** can take the DAA and join training (KB §3–4).
