A one-page synthesis of the EGD Program: who runs it, what it offers each user group, how the pieces connect, and key operational facts.

## Who runs it

The [[egd-program]] is a free, non-profit national programme. It was designed in the 2023 Program Book of the [[digital-economy-business-committee]], which leads and coordinates it, and it is named in Pentagon 5 of the Government's strategy. It is implemented by the [[egd-department]] of [[techo-startup-center]] (TSC), a public administrative institution under the [[ministry-of-economy-and-finance]]. The EGD Department has two offices (Prakas on TSC Offices 2024, Articles 14–15):
- **Policy Research Office** (ការិយាល័យស្រាវជ្រាវគោលនយោបាយ): researches, collects and analyses data on enterprise digital transformation and gives policy recommendations.
- **Digital Capture Office** (ការិយាល័យការចាប់យកឌីជីថល): plans and delivers training and digital adoption support, promotion, cooperation, and also handles the **[[da-grant]]**, **DSP registration** ([[dsp-registration]]), and **the [[egd-platform]]**.

The programme is fully funded by the [[skills-development-fund]] and is currently implementing an ADB-funded MSME project for 2024–2028 ([[adb-msme-digital-adoption-project]]). It supports the "Digital Business" pillar of the [[digital-economy-society-policy-framework]] and Pentagon 5 of the [[pentagonal-strategy-phase-1]].

## The enterprise journey

```
DAA: free, ≈5 min, no login needed (4 sections, 24 scored questions + up to 7 follow-ups)
   → CamDigiKey login
   → DA Training: DA1–DA6, TSC certificate (verify.gov.kh)   (+ E-Learning 24/7 & separate initiatives)
   → DA Grant voucher: $400 formal / $250 informal (size not considered), valid up to 12 months
   → buy a certified DSP solution after voucher issuance: pay the DSP first
   → submit reimbursement request / progress reports → EGD approves in 7–10 working days → KHR transfer via KHQR
```

|---|---|---|

| Log in | [[camdigikey]], [[egd-platform]] | CamDigiKey account required for training and grant applications (app on App Store / Play Store; guide at `https://camdigikey.gov.kh/`) |

## By user group (Brief §1.2)

- **Enterprises (MSMEs), formal and informal:** the journey above. Enterprise size (micro, small, medium) is not considered for the grant tier — only registration documents.
- **DSPs:** apply on the platform ([[dsp-registration]]). Do not list DSP or Digital Solution requirements or documents (refer to [[contacts]]); no fixed approval timeframe. All approved solutions listed in the directory are grant-eligible ([[digital-solution-providers]]).
- **Trainers and digital experts:** DSPs registered on the platform and subject matter experts; apply through the EGD Platform and are reviewed by the EGD team ([[trainers-and-digital-experts]]).
- **Development partners:** facilitate and support the ecosystem (KB §2).

## Cross-cutting rules

- **Everything is free** to enterprises and DSPs (KB §1; FAQ 3.3).
- **Figures are quoted exactly:** [[official-figures]].
- **Khmer uses Glossary terms only:** [[glossary]].
- **Data:** see [[platform-data-privacy]]. DAA results are seen only by the owner and the EGD technical team. Grant-solution data belongs to the business.
