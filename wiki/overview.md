A one-page synthesis of the EGD Program: who runs it, what it offers each user group, how the pieces connect, and where the sources are still unsettled.

## Who runs it

The [[egd-program]] is a free, non-profit national programme. It is implemented by the [[egd-department]] of [[techo-startup-center]] (TSC), a public administrative institution under the [[ministry-of-economy-and-finance]]. It is fully funded by the [[skills-development-fund]] and is currently implementing an ADB-funded MSME project for 2024–2028 ([[adb-msme-digital-adoption-project]]). It supports the "Digital Business" pillar of the [[digital-economy-society-policy-framework]] and Pentagon 5 of the [[pentagonal-strategy-phase-1]].

## The enterprise journey

```
CamDigiKey login
   → DAA: free, ≈5 min, tailored recommendations
   → DA Training: DA1–DA6, TSC certificate        (+ E-Learning, 24/7)
   → DA Grant voucher: $400 formal / $250 informal, valid up to 12 months
   → buy a certified DSP solution: pay the DSP first
   → progress reports (from 3 months) → TSC reimburses
```

## By user group (Brief §1.2)

- **Enterprises (MSMEs), formal and informal:** the journey above. Informal businesses have DA6 and the $250 grant tier. Startups are welcome (FAQ 6.1).
- **DSPs:** register ([[dsp-registration]]), get verified, and are chosen under the grant. Bound by the [[da-grant-general-conditions]].
- **Trainers and digital experts:** teach DA courses and advise enterprises ([[trainers-and-digital-experts]]).
- **Development partners:** named as a user group, but the sources say little about them beyond "facilitate and support the ecosystem" (KB §2).

## Cross-cutting rules

- **Everything is free** to enterprises and DSPs (KB §1; FAQ 3.3).
- **Figures are quoted exactly:** [[official-figures]].
- **Khmer uses Glossary terms only:** [[glossary]].
- **Data:** see [[platform-data-privacy]]. DAA results are seen only by the owner and the EGD technical team. Grant-solution data belongs to the business.

## Still unsettled

- **Open conflicts** ([[source-conflicts]]):
  - "five priority activities" vs three named supports;
  - the hotline numbers;
  - whether the two non-DA programmes count as DA Training;
  - the Guidebook's single 400-dollar cap vs the 2026 $250 informal tier.
- **Missing documents** ([[source-gaps]]): the EGD Program Book and the Platform SOP 2026 are the biggest.
- **Approval:** the knowledge base itself awaits management approval, so every content page is `draft`.
