The official EGD English–Khmer terms (Glossary, 2026-09-11, updated to reflect current platform naming). The chatbot must use these Khmer terms and no others.

**Sources:** `raw/EGD/2026/2026-09-11_Glossary_EGD-Chatbot-Glossary.md` and `raw/EGD/2026/2026-11-08_KnowledgeBase_EGD-Chatbot-Knowledge-Base-Additions.md` (which adds 1.6 Policy Research Office, 1.7 Digital Capture Office, DAA result levels, and updates 3.1, 4.9, and 4.10 to match the live platform).

**Language rule** (Project Brief §1.3.2): the chatbot replies only in the user's main language, never mixing Khmer and English; Khmer answers use only these terms, and never contain Thai or Lao characters. Technical terms such as SaaS, CRM and POS are written "Khmer (English)".

## Notes on the glossary

### Live platform naming alignments

- **1.6 & 1.7 (EGD Department offices):** Added **Policy Research Office** (`ការិយាល័យស្រាវជ្រាវគោលនយោបាយ`) and **Digital Capture Office** (`ការិយាល័យការចាប់យកឌីជីថល`).
- **3.1 (Digital Adoption Assessment):** Official Khmer term is **`ការវាយតម្លៃកម្រិតនៃការចាប់យកឌីជីថល (DAA)`** (using `នៃការ` as on the live platform). Added the 4 DAA result levels (3.6–3.9): `កម្រិតមិនទាន់ប្រើប្រាស់ឌីជីថល`, `កម្រិតមូលដ្ឋាន`, `កម្រិតមធ្យម`, `កម្រិតខ្ពស់`.
- **4.9 & 4.10 (Other training programmes):** **Digital Technology Capacity Training** is **`ពង្រឹងសមត្ថភាពឌីជីថលសម្រាប់សហគ្រិន`** and **Tourism Digital Adoption Initiative** is **`យុទ្ធនាការចាប់យកឌីជីថលសម្រាប់សហគ្រាសក្នុងវិស័យទេសចរណ៍`** (matching the live platform).
