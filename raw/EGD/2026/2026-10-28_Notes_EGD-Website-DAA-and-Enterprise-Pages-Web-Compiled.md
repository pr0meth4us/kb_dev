- **Duration:** results "in only 5 minutes" ("ក្នុងរយៈពេលតែ ៥ នាទី"). Confidence: medium.

- `/daa/survey` itself was not fetched, so whether it asks for login was not checked.

## 2. https://enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/enterprise

- Describes EGD as helping businesses, especially MSMEs, adopt digital technology. Confidence: medium.
- **Benefits listed:**
  - avoiding loss of value and demand as technology changes;
  - adding value to products and services to expand into markets;
  - improving efficiency, sustainability, productivity and competitiveness;
  - becoming an active participant in the digital economy.

  Confidence: medium.
- **"Steps to digital transformation"** (confidence: medium):
  1. Assess digital readiness on the platform, and get information and help from the "enterprise digital transformation center".
  2. Find digital solutions and providers that match the readiness level.
  3. Choose digital products or services.
  4. Receive digital adoption training.
  5. Receive financial and non-financial support.
- **Login:** enterprises log in with a **CAMDIGIKEY** account to join the programme. Confidence: medium.
- The page doesn't link the assessment tool and shows no "coming soon" notice.
- Contact as on the home page: +855 87 955 888; BDC Building, 11th floor, OCIC Boulevard, Chroy Changvar, Phnom Penh.
