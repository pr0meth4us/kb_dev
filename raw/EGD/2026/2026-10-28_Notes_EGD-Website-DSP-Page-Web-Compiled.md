## Who can register

The page defines a DSP as a company, startup or other suitably qualified provider: "អ្នកផ្ដល់ដំណោះស្រាយឌីជីថល គឺជាក្រុមហ៊ុន ឬ ធុរកិច្ចថ្មី…".

Conditions:
1. Be a properly and legally registered company or enterprise ("ជាក្រុមហ៊ុន ឬ សហគ្រាសដែលបានចុះបញ្ជីត្រឹមត្រូវ"), or a suitably qualified startup.
2. Offer correct, clear, easy-to-use digital solutions with clearly defined pricing.
3. Have experience providing digital solutions.
4. Maintain customer data security and privacy.
5. Other conditions apply (no details given).

## Benefits (8 listed)

1. Collaborate on projects and initiatives under the programme.
2. Help enterprises of all sizes take an active part in the digital economy.
3. Wider market reach through enterprises seeking digital solutions.
4. Partner with the programme to support enterprise digital adoption (references the Cambodia Digital Economy and Society Policy Framework 2021–2035).
5. Take part in a programme initiated by Techo Startup Center, a public administrative institution under the MEF.
6. Network with businesses, government agencies and customers.
7. Showcase digital technology and innovation.
8. Join knowledge-sharing sessions, forums and events.

## Required documents ("ឯកសារត្រូវភ្ជាប់")

- Professional licence (if any)
- Patent tax card
- Commercial registration certificate ("វិញ្ញាបនបត្របញ្ចាក់ការចុះបញ្ជីពាណិជ្ជកម្ម")
- Business opening notice (for those registered after 14 June 2020)
- Detailed company information
