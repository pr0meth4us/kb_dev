## KB §5 Digital Adoption (DA) Grant

*Note: There is no new edition of the Grant Guidebook; however, practical implementation has diverged significantly from the guidebook. The knowledge base reflects current operational reality.*


### Grant Amounts


| **Enterprise Type** | **Maximum Grant Amount** |
| --- | --- |
| Formal Economy (Registered) | $400 (or 1,600,000 Riels) |
| Informal Economy (Unregistered) | $250 (or 1,000,000 Riels) |


### Application Rules


| **Item** | **Details** | **Source** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application deadline | No fixed deadline; vouchers depend on available funding. | Team Knowledge; [Grant Guidebook para 11](https://enterprisedigital.gov.kh/pdfs/voucher-scheme-guideline.pdf) |
| Solution bought before the voucher was issued | No. Only solutions bought after the voucher is issued can be claimed. | Team Knowledge |
| One owner with two businesses or shops | One business may apply for more than one voucher for different digital solutions, if the combined value does not exceed the maximum. An owner may apply separately for each business, if each business meets the conditions. | Team Knowledge |
| Required documents (registered enterprises) | Certificate of Incorporation or One-Window licence, and proof of at least 6 (six) months of business operation as of the application date. A Digital Adoption Plan is encouraged. | [Grant Guidebook para 9](https://enterprisedigital.gov.kh/pdfs/voucher-scheme-guideline.pdf); Team Knowledge (document names) |
| *Enterprise size and tier | Micro, small and medium size is not considered. Registered status is regardless of size; it is based on the documents the enterprise has. | Team Knowledge |
| Expired or revoked voucher | **Yes, the enterprise can re-apply** if its voucher expires or is revoked. | Team Knowledge |
| Meaning of "other conditions apply" (`GB ¶9`) | Standard administrative flexibility clause (similar to *"agenda subject to change"*), not a hidden requirement. | Team Knowledge |

| Payment method | Money transfer by the Finance team to the enterprise via KHQR. | Team Knowledge |
| Payment currency | All transactions are in Khmer Riel (KHR) only. EGD does not make payments in US dollars. | Team Knowledge |
| Reimbursement review time | EGD reviews the reimbursement request and gives approval in 7 to 10 working days. | Team Knowledge |
