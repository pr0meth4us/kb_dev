## 1. About EGD Program

The Enterprises Go Digital (EGD) Program is a national initiative implemented by the Enterprises Go Digital Department of Techo Startup Center (TSC), under the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF).

#### Purpose

The EGD Program aims to strengthen the digital capabilities of Cambodian enterprises so they can play a more active role in the digital economy and business. It supports micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) and informal economy actors through training, digital tools, and financial support.

#### Policy Alignment

The EGD Program is part of:

- The “Digital Business” pillar of the Cambodia Digital Economy and Society Policy Framework 2021–2035

- Pentagon 5 (Side 2) of the Pentagonal Strategy – Phase I of the Royal Government of Cambodia

#### Key Facts

- Non-profit public administrative initiative

- Fully funded by the Skills Development Fund (SDF)

- No enrollment fees or commissions are charged to enterprises or Digital Solution Providers (DSPs)

- Currently implementing the project “Supporting the Adoption of Digital Technologies and Fostering Innovation in Micro, Small, and Medium-Sized Enterprises” funded by the Asian Development Bank (ADB) (2024–2028)

#### Priority Activities

The EGD Program has five priority activities.

For full details, please refer to the official EGD Program Book.

## 2. EGD Platform

The Enterprises Go Digital (EGD) Platform (enterprisedigital.gov.kh) is the national digital platform developed under the EGD Program. It serves as a one-stop digital ecosystem that connects enterprises with certified Digital Solution Providers (DSPs) and digital experts based on each enterprise’s level of digital readiness.

#### Purpose of the Platform

The EGD Platform was established to address three key challenges commonly faced by Cambodian enterprises in their digital transformation journey:

- Limited access to information on qualified digital solutions and service providers

- Limited financial resources for investing in digital technologies and tools

- Limited access to professional digital advisory and support services

#### Main Functions

Enterprises can use the EGD Platform to:

- Conduct the Digital Adoption Assessment (DAA)

- Apply for Digital Adoption (DA) Training courses

- Apply for Digital Adoption (DA) Grant

- Discover and connect with certified Digital Solution Providers (DSPs)

- Access E-Learning courses

#### Key Users of the Platform

| User Type | Role on the Platform |
|---|---|
| Enterprises (MSMEs) | Seek Training, Grants, and Digital Solutions |
| Digital Solution Providers (DSPs) | Offer Verified Digital Products and Services |
| Trainers / Digital Experts | Provide Training and Advisory Support |
| Institutions & Partners | Facilitate and Support the Ecosystem |

#### How to Access

- Website: enterprisedigital.gov.kh

- Login requires a CamDigiKey account

## 3. Digital Adoption Assessment (DAA)

The Digital Adoption Assessment (DAA) is a free online tool available on the EGD Platform. It helps enterprises quickly and simply evaluate their current level of digital maturity.

#### Key Features

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Duration | Approximately 5 minutes |
| Cost | Free |
| Purpose | Assess the Enterprise’s Digital Readiness and Maturity |
| Result | Provides Tailored Recommendations based on Industry Type, Enterprise Size (formal/informal), and Location |
| Access | Available on the EGD Platform after Logging in with CamDigiKey |

#### Who Can Use It

- Formal and Informal Enterprises (MSMEs)

- Business Owners and Self-Employed Individuals

#### Benefits

- Understand the current digital maturity level of the business

- Receive practical recommendations for the next steps in digital adoption

- Prepare for Digital Adoption (DA) Training and Digital Adoption (DA) Grant applications

#### Note

The DAA is the first recommended step before applying for Training or Grants on the EGD Platform.

## 4. Digital Adoption (DA) Training

Digital Adoption (DA) Training is a structured training program under the EGD Program. It helps enterprises understand digital solutions and choose the most suitable tools based on their Digital Readiness Level. The program covers both Formal and Informal Economy Businesses.

#### Purpose

- Equip enterprises with knowledge and awareness of digital solutions

- Enable businesses to make informed decisions about digital adoption

- Support both formal and informal enterprises in their digital transformation journey

#### The Six DA Training Activities

| Code | Name | Target Group |
|---|---|---|
| DA1 | A Day in Digital Transformation | All MSMEs |
| DA2 | Kickstart Digital Journey | Early-stage Enterprises |
| DA3 | Integrate into the Value Chain | Enterprises already using some digital tools |
| DA4 | Strengthen Competitive Edge | Mature enterprises ready for advanced technology |
| DA5 | Time to Go Digital | All MSMEs and DSPs |
| DA6 | Digital Adoption for the Informal Economy | Street Vendors, Market Stalls, Self-Employed |

#### Key Benefits

- Official training from industry experts and certified DSPs

- Official certificate issued by Techo Startup Center

- Networking opportunities with Digital Solution Providers (DSPs)

- Eligibility to apply for Digital Adoption (DA) Grant

#### How to Register

- Create a CamDigiKey account

- Log in to the EGD Platform (enterprisedigital.gov.kh)

- Select the desired DA Training course and submit the application

- Wait for approval (usually 1–2 working days)

#### Additional Options

- E-Learning: Self-paced online courses available 24/7 on the EGD Platform

- Women’s participation is highly encouraged

Website: enterprisedigital.gov.kh/digital-adoption-program

## 5. Digital Adoption (DA) Grant

The Digital Adoption (DA) Grant provides financial support to help enterprises adopt digital solutions. Business owners receive a voucher that can be used to purchase certified digital tools and receive end-user training.

#### Grant Amounts

| Enterprise Type | Maximum Grant Amount |
|---|---|
| Formal Economy (Registered) | $400 (or 1,600,000 Riels) |
| Informal Economy (Unregistered / Micro) | $250 (or 1,000,000 Riels) |

- If the cost of the selected digital solution exceeds the grant limit, the enterprise is responsible for paying the remaining amount.

- An enterprise may apply for more than one voucher, provided that the total value of all vouchers does not exceed the maximum allowable limit.

#### Who Can Apply

- Business owners who want to adopt digital solutions

- Priority is given to:

- Enterprises that have never used digital solutions before

- Enterprises that need digital tools to expand their market

- Enterprises in government priority sectors

#### Eligible Digital Solutions

- Digital Marketing

- E-payment

- Accounting & Taxation

- Point of Sale

- Human Resource Management

- Inventory Management

- E-commerce

- Logistics

- ERP

- Chatbot for MSMEs

- Customer Service Management

Note: The DA Grant cannot be used for hardware, equipment, network infrastructure, or human resources.

#### How to Apply

- Click “Apply for Digital Adoption Grant” on the EGD Platform

- Fill in business information and upload required documents

- Select the preferred digital solution

- State the reason for choosing the solution

- Submit the application (processing time: maximum 2 working days)

- Receive the voucher (valid for up to 12 months)

#### Important Conditions

- The enterprise pays the Digital Solution Provider (DSP) first

- After implementation and progress report, Techo Startup Center (TSC) reimburses the enterprise

- Progress reports must be submitted to TSC

Website: enterprisedigital.gov.kh/digital-adoption-grant

## 6. Digital Solution Providers (DSPs)

Digital Solution Providers (DSPs) are companies, startups, or qualified providers that offer digital solutions to enterprises. They register on the EGD Platform to connect with businesses that need digital tools and services.

#### Who Can Become a DSP

To register as a Digital Solution Provider, the organization must:

- Be a legally registered company, enterprise, or qualified startup

- Offer clear, accurate, and user-friendly digital solutions with a transparent pricing model

- Have experience in providing digital solutions

- Comply with customer data security and privacy standards

- Meet other conditions set by the EGD Program

#### Benefits of Joining as a DSP

- Collaborate on projects under the EGD Program

- Expand market reach by connecting with enterprises seeking digital solutions

- Showcase digital products and innovations on the national platform

- Network with businesses, government agencies, and other stakeholders

- Participate in knowledge-sharing sessions, forums, and events

- Contribute to the digital transformation of Cambodian enterprises

#### Required Documents for Registration

- Business Registration Certificate

- Patent Tax Card

- Enterprise Announcement (for companies registered after 14 June 2020)

- Professional license (if any)

- Company details and portfolio of digital solutions

#### How to Register

- Visit the EGD Platform (enterprisedigital.gov.kh)

- Go to the Digital Solution Provider section

- Click Register and submit the required information and documents

- Wait for review and approval by the EGD team

Website: enterprisedigital.gov.kh/ecosystem/service-provid
